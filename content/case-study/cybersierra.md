## The challenge

In a category where buyers Google "[category] vs [incumbent]" before they even open a sales call, organic visibility is table stakes. CyberSierra needed to show up — and rank — for the queries that lead to demos.

Four specific problems stood in the way:

- **Limited organic visibility despite being an established player** — the business was leaning heavily on events for lead generation instead.
- **No topical authority in the cybersecurity space**, which kept the site from ranking for high-intent keywords.
- **An ineffective content strategy** — content gaps and poor lead-conversion rates across what existed.
- **Underperforming blog content** that wasn't generating consistent organic traffic.

## Our approach

Topical authority programme: compliance frameworks, control mappings, audit prep playbooks. Each cluster anchored by a BOFU comparison or alternative page. Technical SEO foundations rebuilt in parallel to make sure the content could compound. We ran this across four phases.

### Phase 1 — Keyword research & content planning

Extensive keyword research identified high-intent business terms, backed by a competitor gap analysis in Ahrefs. Consistent publication against that plan built topical authority quickly and confirmed that high-intent keywords were driving the more qualified leads.

### Phase 2 — On-page SEO optimization

Systematic on-page work — meta tag audits, content-structure fixes — targeted the easiest wins first, pulling immediate traffic out of pages that had been quietly underperforming.

### Phase 3 — Content audit & optimization

A full content-gap analysis fed a pillar-cluster content model. Regular audits confirmed that in-depth, educational content was what built trust and converted visitors into signups — not just volume.

### Phase 4 — Technical SEO enhancement

Technical fixes ensured optimal crawling and indexing, giving the content strategy a foundation that could hold as authority and traffic scaled.

Trust-signal hygiene ran throughout all four phases: clear authorship, citations, schema, and conversion paths matching how compliance buyers actually evaluate vendors.

## The results

Through this strategy, CyberSierra transformed from an event-dependent business into one with a strong organic presence in its category. **Organic traffic grew 279%** over the engagement, in just six months — with the wins concentrated in BOFU and comparison queries, the ones most directly tied to demo conversion in a high-trust category.

Two numbers underline the shift: **keywords ranking in the Top 10 grew from 6 to 338**, and **organic traffic from the US grew 442%** — the market CyberSierra most needed to win.
