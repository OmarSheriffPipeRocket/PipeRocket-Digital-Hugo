## The challenge

Organic was a fraction of where it needed to be. The team had aggressive growth targets and a strong product, but the search footprint wasn't matching the brand's ambition.

Five specific issues were holding organic back:

- **Stagnant website traffic** — no meaningful growth in organic visitors.
- **Content gaps** — the site lacked the relevant, engaging content needed to drive traffic.
- **AI-generated content without SEO strategy** — much of the existing content was AI-written, but without the optimisation or strategic planning needed to actually rank.
- **Ineffective keyword strategy** — the missing link behind the stagnant traffic: no strong keyword plan tying content to demand.
- **No topical authority** — the combination of the above meant the site had never built real authority in its category, the core driver of organic growth.

## Our approach

Six months. Topical authority programme around DevRev's three buyer personas (product, support, CRM), backed by BOFU comparison pages targeting the specific incumbents DevRev was displacing — built out in four phases.

### Phase 1 — Initial discovery and planning

We started with a full SEO content audit to identify keyword and content-planning gaps, underperforming pages, and audience-targeting misalignment. From there, we ran dedicated keyword research on DevRev's core offering — customer support software — and built a roadmap with measurable milestones to track against.

### Phase 2 — Content planning and implementation

With the keyword research in hand, we built a content plan around detailed briefs, re-optimising existing content to close the gaps we'd found. Meta titles, descriptions, and headers were optimised for high-priority pages, structured data and schema were added for rich snippets, and new BOFU pages were built specifically around comparison and solution-oriented queries.

### Phase 3 — Authority building through content expansion

We leveraged the highest-priority, highest-volume, intent-based keywords to expand the content footprint, then interlinked everything to make discovery easier and drive wider engagement.

### Phase 4 — Conversion optimization

In the final phase, BOFU pages were enhanced with lead magnets, sharper offers, and actionable CTAs — refined continuously against performance data and user behaviour.

## The results

Within six months of the phased strategy, results exceeded expectations. **Organic traffic grew 127% in six months** — better than doubling — and crucially, the new traffic was concentrated in BOFU and high-intent mid-funnel pages, not just informational top-of-funnel content.
