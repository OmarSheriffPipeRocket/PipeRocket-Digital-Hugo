Comparing the top 9 best HubSpot alternatives for 2026 includes 1. ActiveCampaign, 2. Zoho CRM, 3. Salesforce (Agentforce Sales), 4. Freshsales, 5. Pipedrive, 6. EngageBay, 7. Klaviyo, 8. Mailchimp, 9. Keap.

ActiveCampaign and Zoho CRM suit teams that want HubSpot's all-in-one scope at a fraction of the price; Salesforce fits enterprise sales orgs with a dedicated RevOps team; Freshsales and Pipedrive target teams that want a lighter, sales-first CRM without HubSpot's marketing bloat; EngageBay and Keap serve small businesses and solo operators on a tight budget; and Klaviyo and Mailchimp are for teams that only need HubSpot for email and don't want to pay for a full CRM they won't use.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving HubSpot means either paying for marketing-hub features a sales-first CRM never included, or discovering too late that a "budget" pick charges by total contact count the same way HubSpot does. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, review credibility on G2, and how directly it answers the two most-cited reasons buyers leave HubSpot: fast-escalating contact-based pricing and the mandatory $3,000 to $7,000 onboarding fee on its Professional and Enterprise tiers.

## TL;DR

1. **ActiveCampaign:** Best for teams that want HubSpot-level automation depth at a fraction of the cost.
2. **Zoho CRM:** Best for budget-conscious teams that want a broad, affordable suite beyond just CRM.
3. **Salesforce (Agentforce Sales):** Best for enterprise sales orgs that need deep customization and a dedicated RevOps team.
4. **Freshsales:** Best for teams that want enterprise-level CRM features without the enterprise price tag.
5. **Pipedrive:** Best for sales teams that want a visual, pipeline-first CRM without marketing-hub bloat.
6. **EngageBay:** Best for small businesses that want an all-in-one HubSpot substitute on a tight budget.
7. **Klaviyo:** Best for ecommerce brands that need deep email and SMS with native Shopify integration.
8. **Mailchimp:** Best for solo marketers who want simple email marketing without a full CRM.
9. **Keap:** Best for solo entrepreneurs and small service businesses that want CRM plus automation in one place.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool was evaluated using G2 review pages, vendor pricing pages, and company case-study pages, cross-checked against secondary review aggregation since G2's own pages consistently blocked direct automated fetches during this research pass. Where a rating showed meaningful variance across sources (Keap, EngageBay), that variance is flagged rather than presented as a single settled number.

The two factors that mattered most for this category: pricing transparency as contact lists grow, since HubSpot's own reviewers cite fast-escalating costs and a mandatory $3,000 to $7,000 onboarding fee on Professional and Enterprise as the top complaint, and fit against a narrower job than "all-in-one," since several alternatives here (Pipedrive, Klaviyo, Mailchimp) deliberately do less than HubSpot and charge accordingly.

## Why Look for a HubSpot Alternative?

The most cited reason buyers look past HubSpot is pricing that escalates fast as contact lists grow, on top of a mandatory, non-refundable $3,000 onboarding fee at Professional ($890/mo) and $7,000 at Enterprise ($3,600/mo), both due in year one regardless of usage.

The second driver is scope mismatch. HubSpot bundles Marketing, Sales, Service, Content, Commerce, and Data Hubs into one "customer platform," which is a real advantage for teams that want everything connected, but overkill (and overpriced) for a team that only needs a sales pipeline or only needs email marketing. Reviewers also flag limited customization and a steep learning curve as recurring complaints even among teams that stay on the platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best HubSpot alternative?

ActiveCampaign is the strongest pick for automation depth at a lower price. Zoho CRM is the closest match for a broad, affordable all-in-one suite.

### Is there a cheaper HubSpot alternative?

Yes. Zoho CRM, EngageBay, Klaviyo, and Mailchimp offer free tiers, and Freshsales starts at $9/user/mo, well below HubSpot's paid tiers.

### Does HubSpot really charge an onboarding fee?

Yes. HubSpot Professional requires a non-refundable $3,000 onboarding fee in year one and Enterprise $7,000, both on top of the monthly subscription.

### Which HubSpot alternative is best for ecommerce brands?

Klaviyo, thanks to its deep Shopify integration and segmentation; named client ICONIC London reported a 12.5x ROI after switching to it.

### Which HubSpot alternative works best for enterprise sales teams?

Salesforce (Agentforce Sales), rated G2's #1 Best Software Product overall in 2025, though it needs a dedicated RevOps team to run properly.

### Which HubSpot alternative has a genuinely free plan?

Zoho CRM, EngageBay, Klaviyo, and Mailchimp publish real free tiers. Freshsales no longer does; it offers a 21-day trial instead.

### Which alternative is closest to HubSpot's all-in-one model?

Zoho CRM and EngageBay both bundle CRM, marketing, and support functions in one suite, similar in scope to HubSpot but at a lower price point.
