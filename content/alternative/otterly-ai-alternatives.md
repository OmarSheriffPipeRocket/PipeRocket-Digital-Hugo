Comparing the top 8 best Otterly.AI alternatives for 2026 includes 1. Profound, 2. Peec AI, 3. AthenaHQ, 4. Scrunch AI, 5. Rankscale, 6. Goodie AI, 7. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, 8. Ahrefs Brand Radar.

Profound suits enterprise and growth-stage teams that have outgrown Otterly's prompt caps and want deeper agent-citation reporting; Peec AI fits budget-conscious teams that want flexible engine choice, including Google AI Mode and Grok, plus unlimited seats at a $95 entry price; AthenaHQ works for teams that want real multi-engine coverage at zero cost before paying anything; Scrunch AI targets teams that want persona-based agent-experience monitoring backed by a real enterprise client roster; Rankscale suits teams chasing the lowest entry price with no per-engine surcharge; Goodie AI is for teams that want built-in content-optimization actions alongside tracking; the Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit fits teams that want AI tracking either standalone or bundled into a wider SEO suite; and Ahrefs Brand Radar is the pick for teams that want the widest answer-surface view, including YouTube, TikTok, and Reddit.

Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, engine coverage per dollar, and review credibility on G2, since several listings in this category are too thin to treat as statistically meaningful. Worth knowing before you switch: most tools in this category, including several below, cap included engines and charge per extra model, so read the engine column carefully rather than the headline price.

## Why Look for an Otterly.AI Alternative?

The most cited reason buyers look elsewhere is prompt-and-engine math: Otterly charges per prompt per engine, so tracking 100 prompts across 5 engines consumes 500 capture events, not 100, a common source of bill-shock as teams scale coverage. The $189/mo Standard tier ceiling arrives quickly once a team covers multiple product lines, regions, or competitor sets, with a realistic working configuration landing at $300+ once Gemini, AI Mode, and Claude add-ons are stacked on.

The second driver is engine gating itself. Only 4 engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, MS Copilot) are included at any Otterly tier; Google AI Mode, Gemini, and Claude are paid add-ons costing $9-$439/mo each depending on plan. The third reason is dashboard clutter, the most common usability gripe reviewers cite once teams outgrow the Lite tier.
