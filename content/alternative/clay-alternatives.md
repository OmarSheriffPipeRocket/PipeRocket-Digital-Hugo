Comparing the top 9 best Clay alternatives for 2026 includes 1. Apollo.io, 2. Unify (UnifyGTM), 3. ZoomInfo, 4. Cognism, 5. Lusha, 6. Surfe, 7. Warmly, 8. Clearbit (Breeze Intelligence), 9. PhantomBuster.

Apollo.io and Unify fit teams that want one self-serve vendor instead of Clay's orchestration layer over 130+ providers; ZoomInfo and Cognism suit enterprise and EMEA-focused teams that need the deepest verified data and can sign an annual contract; Lusha and Surfe target SMB and LinkedIn-first prospecting teams on tighter budgets; Warmly serves inbound-intent and website-deanonymization use cases; and Clearbit and PhantomBuster work best layered onto an existing HubSpot subscription or LinkedIn workflow rather than as full replacements.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving Clay means paying five-figure annual minimums for data depth a smaller vendor already covers, or trading Clay's multi-provider waterfall for a single-source database with real accuracy gaps. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, review credibility, and how directly it answers the two most-cited reasons buyers look past Clay: unpredictable credit-based billing and a learning curve steep enough that some teams hire a dedicated "Clay expert."

## TL;DR

1. **Apollo.io:** Best for teams that want one all-in-one vendor with its own contact database instead of an orchestration layer.
2. **Unify (UnifyGTM):** Best for teams that want an AI-native, chat-driven GTM platform built around agents, the closest philosophical match to Clay.
3. **ZoomInfo:** Best for enterprise teams that need the deepest first-party contact and org-chart database plus intent data.
4. **Cognism:** Best for EMEA-focused teams that need phone-verified mobile numbers and GDPR-compliant coverage.
5. **Lusha:** Best for SMB and mid-market teams that want a lightweight browser-extension workflow at a lower price than ZoomInfo.
6. **Surfe:** Best for teams that prospect inside LinkedIn and Sales Navigator and want one-click capture into their CRM.
7. **Warmly:** Best for teams that want to de-anonymize website visitors and trigger outreach automatically instead of building cold lists.
8. **Clearbit (Breeze Intelligence):** Best for teams already running HubSpot that want enrichment built natively into existing workflows.
9. **PhantomBuster:** Best for teams that want no-code LinkedIn scraping and list-building automation as a companion tool.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool was evaluated using G2 and FeaturedCustomers listings, vendor pricing pages, and funding/company databases (Crunchbase, PitchBook). G2's product pages consistently blocked direct automated fetches during this research pass, so ratings were cross-checked against independently indexed search results citing the same figures rather than a single unverifiable page, and any unconfirmed figure is flagged in the card below rather than presented as solid evidence.

The two factors that mattered most for this category: pricing transparency and predictability, since Clay's dual-currency "actions plus data credits" model and its 30 percent mid-cycle top-up premium are the most cited reasons buyers look elsewhere, and data-model fit, since a single-source contact database solves a different problem than Clay's multi-provider waterfall enrichment.

Apollo's own 240M+ contact database and 600,000+ reported company users give it a scale few single-tool competitors match, and its per-seat pricing is published, not quote-gated like ZoomInfo or Cognism.

## Why Look for a Clay Alternative?

The most cited reason buyers look past Clay is its dual-currency pricing model. "Actions" and "data credits" bill separately, failed or no-match enrichments still burn credits, and mid-cycle top-ups carry a 30 percent premium, a combination that makes monthly spend hard to forecast at scale.

The second driver is the learning curve. Building reliable, cost-efficient workflows in Clay often requires a dedicated "Clay expert" or data-ops hire, and G2 reviewers describe it as complex enough to delay time-to-value. Recent 2025-2026 pricing changes have also reportedly made it less accessible to solo operators and small teams running high-frequency enrichment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Clay alternative?

Apollo.io is the strongest all-in-one pick for most teams. Unify is the closest match for teams that want Clay's AI-agent philosophy in a tighter product.

### Is there a cheaper Clay alternative?

Yes. Unify starts at $20/seat/mo and Surfe at $39/user/mo, both well under Clay's $167/mo Launch tier for a comparable feature set.

### Which Clay alternative has a free plan?

Apollo.io, Unify, Lusha, and Surfe all offer a free tier or free trial. ZoomInfo, Cognism, and Warmly require a sales demo instead.

### Which alternative is closest to Clay's AI-agent approach?

Unify is the closest match, built around AI-driven "Plays" and intent triggers rather than a static contact database.

### Which alternative is best for enterprise teams?

ZoomInfo offers the deepest first-party database and org-chart data, though its five-figure annual minimums require enterprise-level budget.

### Which alternative works natively inside HubSpot?

Clearbit, now rebranded Breeze Intelligence, bills through HubSpot's own credit system and requires an active paid HubSpot plan.

### Does Clay offer a free trial?

Clay has a Free plan at $0/mo with 500 actions and 100 data credits/mo, rather than a time-limited trial of paid tiers.
