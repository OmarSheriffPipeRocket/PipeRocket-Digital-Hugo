Comparing the top 7 best AB Tasty alternatives in 2026 includes 1. VWO, 2. Kameleoon, 3. Convert.com, 4. Optimizely, 5. Omniconvert, 6. PostHog, and 7. Unbounce.

VWO fits teams that want AB Tasty's full breadth of testing, personalization, and analytics without an enterprise-only sales cycle; Kameleoon suits teams that want the same AI-driven predictive experimentation with an actual published starting price; Convert.com is built for agencies and cost-conscious teams that want transparent tiers instead of a quote; Optimizely fits enterprise buyers consolidating experimentation into a single CMS and commerce platform; Omniconvert works for ecommerce teams that want a genuinely free entry tier; PostHog is for engineering-led SaaS teams running product experiments rather than marketing-led site personalization; and Unbounce fits teams whose testing need centers on landing pages and campaigns.

Picking the wrong swap means paying AB Tasty-level fees for a tool with less capability, or landing on something so light it can't replace the personalization and feature-experimentation depth a team actually used. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, self-serve access, feature parity with AB Tasty's testing and personalization stack, and what verified reviewers on G2 and Capterra say about running it day to day.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool on this list was evaluated using Capterra listings, G2 review aggregates, and vendor pricing pages, since G2 blocks direct automated fetches of its review pages site-wide. Two ratings here (VWO, Unbounce) were confirmed via direct Capterra fetch; every G2 figure cited is sourced from a search-cache snippet and should be rechecked live before you rely on it.

The two factors that separated this shortlist: pricing transparency (AB Tasty's quote-only model with no public number is the most commonly cited reason buyers start looking elsewhere, so alternatives needed either a published starting price or a genuine free tier) and self-serve access (a trial or free account you can start today, rather than a sales-gated proof-of-concept, matching the exact friction AB Tasty buyers report).

## Why Look for an AB Tasty Alternative?

AB Tasty covers web and feature experimentation, personalization, and e-merchandising for enterprise brands including Clarins, Ulta, Samsonite, Sephora, and L'Oréal. It holds a reported 4.4/5 across 410 G2 reviews (unverified via direct fetch; recheck live) and a verified 4.6/5 on Capterra, though that Capterra score comes from only 11 reviews, too thin a sample to treat as statistically meaningful on its own.

The most commonly cited reason teams look elsewhere is pricing. AB Tasty's model is fully custom and quote-based, with Capterra listing a reference starting price of €15,000 a year and no self-serve tier at any level. Buyers also report that the implementation and services overhead around onboarding is heavy for teams that just want to run experiments quickly rather than commission a managed program.

That combination, a five-figure annual floor plus a sales-led rollout, is what pushes teams toward lighter, self-serve, or usage-based alternatives: a published starting price (Kameleoon, Convert.com, Unbounce), a genuine free tier (Omniconvert, PostHog), or at minimum a real trial that starts without a sales call (VWO). Optimizely is the exception on this list: it matches AB Tasty's quote-only pricing model, so it only makes sense for teams whose real frustration was tool sprawl rather than cost.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best AB Tasty alternative?

VWO is the closest overall match: it covers the same testing, personalization, and analytics ground AB Tasty does, but opens with a genuine 30-day self-serve trial instead of a sales-gated demo.

### Is there a free AB Tasty alternative?

Omniconvert's Explore plan is free permanently for up to 50,000 tested users a month with full feature access. PostHog's Experiments module is also free for the first 1 million flag requests a month.

### What is the cheapest AB Tasty alternative with a published price?

Unbounce starts at $29 a month, though its scope is limited to landing pages. For full-site testing with a published number, Convert.com starts at $299 to $399 a month and Kameleoon starts at $495 a month.

### Why do teams leave AB Tasty?

The two most cited reasons are pricing (a reported €15,000-a-year floor with no self-serve tier) and heavy implementation overhead for teams that want to run experiments quickly rather than commission a managed rollout.

### Is Optimizely cheaper than AB Tasty?

No. Optimizely uses the same fully custom, quote-based pricing model as AB Tasty, with no published numbers on its pricing page. It's a fit for tool consolidation rather than cost savings.

### Does AB Tasty offer a free trial?

No public self-serve or free trial tier is listed on AB Tasty's site; access starts with a custom quote. Several alternatives here, including VWO, Kameleoon, and Omniconvert, offer a genuine trial or free tier instead.

### Which AB Tasty alternative is best for engineering-led SaaS teams?

PostHog is built specifically for engineering-led teams running product experiments, with feature flags and analytics in the same platform, rather than a marketing-facing personalization tool like AB Tasty.
