Comparing the top 8 best Scrunch AI alternatives in 2026 includes 1. AthenaHQ, 2. Profound, 3. Otterly.AI, 4. Peec AI, 5. Rankscale, 6. Ahrefs Brand Radar, 7. Goodie AI, 8. Bluefish AI.

AthenaHQ suits teams that want visibility gaps turned into assignable fixes instead of a static dashboard; Profound targets enterprise and growth-stage brands that want the deepest analytics and the largest reviewed platform in the category; Otterly.AI fits budget-conscious SaaS and agency teams that want a real free trial before paying; Peec AI is built for funded B2B teams that want direct founder access and a low-cost, pick-your-models entry tier; Rankscale serves teams chasing the widest engine coverage at the lowest entry price; Ahrefs Brand Radar suits marketing teams already inside the Ahrefs suite who want a historical AI-visibility index; Goodie AI targets mid-market teams that want monitoring and content execution bundled into one workflow; and Bluefish AI is built for Fortune 500 marketing organizations that have outgrown a mid-market self-serve tool entirely.

Scrunch AI's entry Starter plan runs $250/mo on annual billing ($300 month-to-month) for 350 custom prompts across six platforms, and pushes expanded prompt volume plus its deeper observability features into a custom-priced Enterprise tier, so picking the wrong replacement can mean paying similar money for a tool that still only monitors instead of helping fix what it finds. Each alternative below was evaluated on engine coverage relative to price, whether the tool moves past monitoring into action, and what G2, Capterra, and vendor pricing pages actually confirm.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Every price and plan detail below was read off the vendor's own live pricing page in July 2026, and every rating is attributed to the review platform it came from.
