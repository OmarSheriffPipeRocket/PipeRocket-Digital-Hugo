Comparing the top 9 best PostHog alternatives for 2026 includes 1. Mixpanel, 2. Amplitude, 3. Heap, 4. Pendo, 5. Statsig, 6. FullStory, 7. Hotjar, 8. LogRocket, 9. Matomo.

Mixpanel and Amplitude suit teams that want a like-for-like self-serve or enterprise product-analytics swap with a startup-friendly free tier; Heap and Pendo fit teams that want automatic no-code tracking or in-app guidance layered on top of analytics; Statsig and LogRocket target engineering-led teams that lean on rigorous experimentation or AI-assisted debugging; FullStory and Hotjar work best for teams that live in session replay and qualitative UX research; and Matomo is built for privacy-first teams that want to self-host and own their data outright.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving PostHog means paying enterprise per-MAU or per-session pricing for a feature set PostHog's usage-based free tier already covered, or trading its analytics-plus-replay-plus-flags bundle for three separate specialist subscriptions. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, review credibility, and how directly it answers the two most-cited reasons buyers look past PostHog: unpredictable usage-based billing spread across ten-plus separately metered products, and wanting a dedicated specialist (replay, experimentation, or adoption) rather than an all-in-one, engineering-centric generalist.

## TL;DR

1. **Mixpanel:** Best for teams that want a near drop-in product-analytics swap with a startup-friendly free tier.
2. **Amplitude:** Best for larger product orgs that want a permanent 2M-event free tier plus enterprise-grade experimentation.
3. **Heap:** Best for teams that want automatic no-code event capture without instrumenting every click.
4. **Pendo:** Best for product and PLG teams that want in-app guidance and adoption tooling bundled with analytics.
5. **Statsig:** Best for teams that want statistically rigorous, engineering-led experimentation as the primary use case.
6. **FullStory:** Best for teams that want the deepest session-replay and DOM-capture fidelity as their core workflow.
7. **Hotjar:** Best for marketing and CRO teams that want heatmaps, surveys, and qualitative feedback over raw event data.
8. **LogRocket:** Best for engineering teams that want AI-assisted session replay for catching production bugs fast.
9. **Matomo:** Best for privacy-first teams that want a self-hosted, open-source platform with full data ownership.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool was evaluated using vendor pricing pages and G2 listings, cross-checked against secondary review-aggregation sources where G2's product pages blocked direct fetches during this research pass. Quotes below come from each vendor's own published case studies rather than raw G2 review text, and are flagged as such in each card.

The two factors that mattered most for this category: pricing transparency (PostHog's own rate card is fully published and usage-based, so alternatives were judged on whether they matched that transparency or hid behind a demo wall) and specialization fit, since most buyers leaving PostHog want either a closer general-purpose swap or a narrower specialist tool, not a vaguer version of the same bundle.

## Why Look for a PostHog Alternative?

The most cited reason buyers look past PostHog is billing complexity. Its usage-based rate card spans ten-plus separately metered products (events, replays, flags, surveys, warehouse rows, logs, AI credits), and while the free tier covers most small teams, forecasting cost at scale across that many meters is harder than a flat per-MAU or per-seat competitor.

The second driver is wanting a dedicated specialist instead of a generalist. Teams that need a CSM-driven enterprise motion, a pure qualitative/session-replay tool, a pure experimentation platform, or a privacy-first self-hosted option often find a narrower, purpose-built tool serves that single need better than PostHog's broad, engineering-centric bundle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best PostHog alternative?

Mixpanel is the closest self-serve swap for most teams; Amplitude suits larger orgs, and Statsig fits teams that prioritize experimentation.

### Is there a free PostHog alternative?

Yes. Mixpanel, Amplitude, Heap, Pendo, Statsig, and FullStory all offer permanent free tiers with real usage allowances.

### Which PostHog alternative is best for session replay?

FullStory offers the deepest replay and DOM-capture fidelity; LogRocket pairs replay with AI-assisted error detection.

### Which alternative is best for enterprise product analytics?

Amplitude, with a permanently free 2M-event tier and an enterprise client roster spanning 30 of the Fortune 100.

### Is there a privacy-first, self-hosted PostHog alternative?

Matomo. Its Community edition is free forever, self-hosted, with full data ownership and no sampling.

### Which PostHog alternative is best for feature experimentation?

Statsig, built by ex-Meta engineers around Facebook's internal experimentation infrastructure, now sold standalone.

### Does PostHog itself offer a free plan?

Yes. PostHog's free tier includes 1M events, 5K replays, and unlimited seats; over 90 percent of teams reportedly use it free.
