Comparing the top 9 best Goodie AI alternatives for 2026 includes 1. Profound, 2. Otterly.AI, 3. Peec AI, 4. Scrunch AI, 5. AthenaHQ, 6. Rankscale, 7. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, 8. Ahrefs Brand Radar, 9. Brandlight.

Profound and Brandlight suit enterprise teams that need SOC2/SSO and white-glove support; Otterly.AI and Rankscale fit budget-conscious teams that want a real trial before committing; Peec AI and AthenaHQ work for mid-market teams that want hands-on vendor access or a genuinely free entry tier; Scrunch AI serves teams worried about AI models stating wrong facts about their brand; and Semrush's and Ahrefs' AI-visibility add-ons fit teams that would rather bolt AI tracking onto a platform they already pay for.

Goodie AI is a genuinely early-stage tool: about 11 people, no independently verified reviews on G2 or Capterra, and a $399-per-user/mo entry price with no published lower tier or free trial. Each alternative below was evaluated on pricing transparency, engine coverage, funding and team depth, and what verified G2 and Capterra listings actually show, since that last point is where Goodie itself currently has nothing to point to. Where a tool genuinely has no review history (Rankscale) or a disputed one (Profound), that is stated outright rather than smoothed over with a single tidy number.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Every price in this comparison was read off the vendor's own live pricing page (or, for Semrush, its own knowledge-base article) on 2026-07-29. G2 blocks automated fetches on every product page it serves, so each rating and review count is cross-checked against G2's own indexed directory text plus independent third-party analyses, and is explicitly flagged as disputed wherever those sources disagree. Where no verifiable named reviewer quote exists for a tool, we say so instead of paraphrasing one. Founding dates, funding, and team size came from named data aggregators (Tracxn, Latka) or vendor press releases, never from unverified affiliate blogs.

The two factors that mattered most here: verified review depth (since Goodie AI itself has zero confirmed G2/Capterra reviews despite a premium price) and engine/model coverage, because Goodie markets "the most robust model coverage in the space" and any real alternative needs to match or beat that claim with evidence.

For the full process (every source we use, what disqualifies a tool, our conflict-of-interest handling, and our corrections policy) read [our research methodology and editorial policy](/research-methodology/).
