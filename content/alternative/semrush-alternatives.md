Comparing the top 8 best Semrush alternatives in 2026 includes 1. Ahrefs, 2. SE Ranking, 3. Moz Pro, 4. Similarweb, 5. Serpstat, 6. Mangools, 7. SpyFu, 8. Ubersuggest.

Ahrefs is the closest match on raw data depth for teams that need the deepest backlink index; SE Ranking is the best-value full platform for teams that want Semrush-equivalent breadth at a lower price; Moz Pro suits agencies that want daily-use simplicity and local SEO tools; Similarweb fits teams that need market-level traffic intelligence rather than keyword-by-keyword tracking; Serpstat is a genuine all-in-one suite at a fraction of Semrush's cost; Mangools and Ubersuggest serve solo marketers and founders on tight budgets; and SpyFu is the pick for teams that lean heavily on PPC competitor intelligence.

The most common reasons buyers look for a Semrush alternative are renewal price hikes, low feature utilization relative to what they're paying for, and cancellation friction, so each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, data depth, and free-plan or trial availability.

For a side-by-side on features and pricing, read our [Moz vs Semrush](/compare/moz-vs-semrush/) comparison, our [SE Ranking vs Semrush](/compare/se-ranking-vs-semrush/) breakdown, or [Mangools vs Semrush](/compare/mangools-vs-semrush/).

## TL;DR

1. **Ahrefs:** Best for SEOs who prioritize backlink analysis and the largest frequently refreshed link index.
2. **SE Ranking:** Best for budget-conscious SaaS teams and agencies that want Semrush-equivalent breadth at a lower price.
3. **Moz Pro:** Best for agencies and growing businesses that want daily-use simplicity and built-in local SEO tools.
4. **Similarweb:** Best for teams that need market-level traffic and competitive intelligence rather than keyword rank tracking.
5. **Serpstat:** Best for freelancers and growing agencies that want genuine all-in-one breadth at a fraction of Semrush's price.
6. **Mangools:** Best for solopreneurs and small SaaS teams who want a simple, low-cost entry point.
7. **SpyFu:** Best for teams running paid search alongside SEO who want combined PPC and SEO competitor intelligence.
8. **Ubersuggest:** Best for solo founders and budget-constrained teams who need basic keyword and backlink visibility.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool was evaluated using G2 and Capterra review data, along with reported buyer sentiment from marketing and SEO practitioner forums on why teams look for a Semrush alternative in the first place (price escalation, cancellation friction, and low feature utilization relative to cost). Pricing was cross-checked against live product pages as of July 2026 where accessible, and secondary sources where a pricing page did not expose numeric tiers to automated verification; those fields are flagged in the relevant card below.

The two criteria that mattered most for this category: pricing transparency at the plan level (since Semrush churn is driven largely by unpredictable renewal increases and per-seat costs), and data depth on the specific axis a buyer cares about (backlinks, rank tracking, or market intelligence), since no single tool matches Semrush's full breadth at a lower price.

### 1. Ahrefs

Best for: SEOs who prioritize backlink analysis and the largest frequently refreshed link index over Semrush's broader feature surface.

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) is an SEO suite built around one of the largest independent backlink indexes in the industry, alongside keyword research, rank tracking, and site audits. It's the most frequently recommended Semrush alternative specifically for link-data depth.

| G2 Rating | [4.5/5 (692 reviews)](https://www.g2.com/products/ahrefs/reviews) |

**Fit Profile**

It's for SEOs and link-building teams whose primary work is backlink analysis and competitor link research, where Semrush's broader (but shallower) all-in-one breadth isn't the priority. Content Explorer and click-metrics in Keywords Explorer are workflow strengths.

It's NOT for teams that want PPC competitor intelligence or a no-commitment trial. Ahrefs offers no free trial as of July 2026, and credit-based usage limits on lower tiers frustrate high-volume users.

**Core Strengths**

Ahrefs' Site Explorer backlink index is widely cited as one of the largest independent web crawlers in the category, and reviewers report 77% rating the product 5 stars on G2. Domain Rating (DR) is a widely used industry shorthand.

- Backlink index cited as one of the largest independent crawlers in the category
- Keywords Explorer with click-metrics goes beyond raw search volume
- 77% of G2 reviewers rate Ahrefs 5 stars, per reviewer summary

**Standout feature:** The Site Explorer backlink index is the core reason teams choose Ahrefs over Semrush for link-focused work. It's the benchmark tool in this set for competitor link-gap analysis.

**What Users Say**

**Love:** "Users consistently praise Ahrefs for its accurate backlink data and comprehensive keyword research, which significantly aid in SEO strategies and competitor analysis," per [G2 review summary](https://www.g2.com/products/ahrefs/reviews).

- 77% of reviewers rate Ahrefs 5 stars, per the same G2 summary.

**Complain:** "Pricing and usage credits are a near-universal complaint"
Pricing and credit-based usage limits are flagged repeatedly, and freelancers in price-sensitive markets cite cost as a barrier despite high satisfaction with data quality, per [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/ahrefs/reviews).

- No free trial as of July 2026 makes pre-purchase evaluation harder than with SE Ranking or Mangools.

**Known Gaps**

Ahrefs has no PPC competitor intelligence to speak of, so teams that need combined paid and organic data should look at SpyFu instead. The Lite plan's credit limits cap how many reports can be run per month, and the Starter tier is very restricted in scope.

- No PPC intelligence; SpyFu or Semrush cover that gap
- Credit and usage limits on lower tiers restrict report volume

**Our Read**

Ahrefs is the right call when backlinks are the reason you're evaluating alternatives to Semrush. If PPC intelligence or all-in-one breadth at a lower price matters more, look further down this list.

## Why Look for a Semrush Alternative?

The most commonly reported reason teams look for a Semrush alternative is price escalation. Business-tier seats and renewals climb fast, and buyers have reported unannounced renewal price hikes, including one cited example of a renewal jumping roughly 68% with no advance notice. That's a real budgeting risk for a SaaS marketing team on a fixed annual plan.

The second driver is low feature utilization. Teams report using roughly a quarter of the suite while paying for the whole bundle, effectively subsidizing features they don't touch just to justify the price tier. Cancellation friction compounds this: Semrush has no self-serve cancel button, requiring a chat request and a reason form instead.

The third reason is the jack-of-all-trades trade-off. Because Semrush spans keyword research, rank tracking, backlinks, content tools, and PPC intelligence all in one product, no single module gets the focused depth a specialist tool like Ahrefs (backlinks) or SpyFu (PPC) provides. The broad feature surface also means new team members need real ramp-up time compared with a purpose-built point tool.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Semrush alternative?

Ahrefs is the strongest alternative for backlink-focused work. SE Ranking is the best-value full platform for teams that want Semrush-equivalent breadth at a lower price with a no-card trial.

### Is Ahrefs better than Semrush?

For backlink depth and link-gap analysis, many reviewers rate Ahrefs higher. Semrush offers broader all-in-one breadth including PPC intelligence. The right pick depends on which gap you're solving for.

### What is the cheapest Semrush alternative?

Ubersuggest is the cheapest at a reported $12/mo, with a lifetime-deal option. Among full all-in-one suites, Mangools at $29.90/mo and Serpstat at $69/mo are lower-cost than Semrush's $139.95/mo Pro plan.

### Why are people leaving Semrush?

Reported drivers include renewal price hikes, low feature utilization relative to cost, cancellation friction (no self-serve cancel button), and a feature surface broad enough that no single module gets specialist-level depth.

### Is SE Ranking a good Semrush replacement?

For most SMBs and small agencies, yes. SE Ranking offers a full platform at a lower price, higher Ease of Use scores than Semrush per G2, and a no-credit-card 14-day trial, though its data depth trails Semrush at enterprise scale.

### Which Semrush alternative has the best backlink data?

Ahrefs is the benchmark in this set for backlink data, widely cited as running one of the largest independent web crawlers in the category.

### Does any Semrush alternative offer a true free plan?

Mangools offers a free version, and Ubersuggest offers a free tier alongside its trial. SE Ranking and Serpstat offer no-cost trials rather than permanent free tiers.
