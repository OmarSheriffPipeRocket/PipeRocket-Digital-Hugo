Comparing the top 7 best Ahrefs alternatives in 2026 includes 1. Semrush, 2. SE Ranking, 3. Moz Pro, 4. SpyFu, 5. Mangools, 6. Serpstat, 7. Screaming Frog.

Semrush is the pick for teams that want a single platform spanning SEO, PPC, content, and AI-search visibility tracking; SE Ranking is the best-value all-in-one for cost-conscious SaaS teams and agencies that want native white-label reporting; Moz Pro suits teams that want a beginner-friendly interface and the long-standing Domain Authority metric; SpyFu fits teams that need combined SEO and PPC competitor intelligence on a budget; Mangools is for solo founders who want simple keyword research without an enterprise price tag; Serpstat targets budget-conscious teams that want feature parity without a credit-based pricing model; and Screaming Frog is the cheapest option for teams that mainly need Ahrefs' crawling and technical-audit function, paired with free Google Search Console data.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving Ahrefs means paying for depth you don't use, or worse, ending up with a smaller database and a similar price tag. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, database size, free-trial availability, and what buyers on G2 and Reddit say drove them away from Ahrefs in the first place: cost increases from the 2024 credit-based pricing shift, credit quotas that create workflow friction, the lack of a free trial, and occasional data-accuracy gaps versus Google Search Console.

1. **Semrush:** Best for teams that want SEO, PPC, content, and AI-search visibility tracking in one platform.

6. **Serpstat:** Best for budget-conscious teams that want Ahrefs-comparable features without a credit-based pricing model.
7. **Screaming Frog:** Best for technical SEO teams that mainly need crawling and site-audit depth at near-zero cost.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool on this list was evaluated using G2 review data, Capterra listings, and secondary sources corroborating pricing and ratings, since G2's product pages block direct automated fetches. Pricing was checked against live vendor pages as of July 2026, and any field that couldn't be confirmed from a primary source is flagged in the relevant card below.

## Why Look for an Ahrefs Alternative?

The most common reason teams leave Ahrefs is cost. The 2024 shift to a credit-based pricing system raised effective prices for many buyers by up to 50 percent, replacing older flat or grandfathered plans, according to reviewer discussion aggregated by SearchAtlas. Credit quotas add friction on top of that: reports and filters consume credits, and the Starter tier has no add-on purchase option when you run out mid-month.

The second driver is the lack of a free trial. Ahrefs doesn't offer one as of this writing, which makes it hard to evaluate before committing, especially compared with SE Ranking's 14-day trial or Mangools' free account tier. Some reviewers on Reddit also report that Ahrefs' traffic and keyword estimates diverge from Google Search Console data, which raises data-accuracy questions for teams that lean on those numbers for reporting.

The third reason is entry-point cost for solo operators and early-stage teams. Ahrefs' Lite tier starts at $129/mo, a steep floor next to Mangools, SE Ranking, or Serpstat, all of which offer comparable core functionality at a fraction of the price for teams that don't need Ahrefs' full database depth.

### What is the cheapest Ahrefs alternative?

Mangools is the cheapest full toolkit at $29.90/mo annual, with a free account tier. Screaming Frog is cheaper still for crawling alone, at roughly $250/yr.

### Does Ahrefs offer a free trial?

No. As of this research pass, Ahrefs does not offer a free trial, which is a commonly cited reason buyers look at SE Ranking or Mangools instead.

### Which Ahrefs alternative has the best backlink data?

Semrush's 43T backlink database is the largest reported in this set, though several reviewers still consider Ahrefs' index the industry benchmark for freshness.

### Is Screaming Frog a full Ahrefs replacement?

No. Screaming Frog only replaces Ahrefs' crawling and Site Audit function. It has no keyword or backlink database and is usually paired with Search Console.
