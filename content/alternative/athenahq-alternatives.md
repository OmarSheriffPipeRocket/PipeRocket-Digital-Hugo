Comparing the top 9 best AthenaHQ alternatives for 2026 includes 1. Profound, 2. Scrunch AI, 3. Peec AI, 4. Otterly.AI, 5. Ahrefs Brand Radar, 6. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, 7. Evertune, 8. RankScale, 9. Relixir.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool was evaluated using G2 and Capterra listings, YC/Tracxn/funding databases, and pricing pulled directly from vendor sites. G2 blocks direct automated fetching, so ratings and review counts were cross-confirmed across multiple independent search-result snippets citing the same figures rather than a single unverifiable source.

The two factors that mattered most for this category: citation-level transparency (since thin citation detail behind AthenaHQ's Enterprise tier is the most cited reason buyers leave) and engine/surface coverage relative to price, since AthenaHQ's own 9-engine Starter tier sets a real baseline to beat. Suite-level ratings (Semrush, Ahrefs) and non-statistical, thin-review vendors (Peec AI, Evertune, RankScale, Relixir) are flagged rather than presented as equivalent evidence. Where a vendor has no aggregated public rating on G2, we say so instead of assigning a score, and we name the directory when the only rating sits elsewhere (RankScale's 4.6/5 comes from OMR Reviews, not G2).

For the full process (every source we use, what disqualifies a tool, our conflict-of-interest handling, and our corrections policy) read [our research methodology and editorial policy](/research-methodology/).
