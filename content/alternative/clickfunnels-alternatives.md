Comparing the top 9 best ClickFunnels alternatives for 2026 includes 1. Systeme.io, 2. Kartra, 3. GoHighLevel, 4. Kajabi, 5. Leadpages, 6. Unbounce, 7. Instapage, 8. GetResponse, 9. Builderall.

Systeme.io suits bootstrapped creators who want a genuinely free starting tier; Kartra fits coaches and course sellers who want funnels, memberships, and a helpdesk under one login; GoHighLevel is built for agencies that want to white-label the platform and resell it as their own SaaS; Kajabi targets course creators and coaches specifically, with an interface built around content delivery rather than checkout funnels; Leadpages suits small businesses that want unlimited landing pages without ClickFunnels' broader (and pricier) feature set; Unbounce fits marketing teams that treat conversion-rate optimization as a dedicated discipline, not just a page builder; Instapage is built for paid-media teams running high-volume campaigns that need post-click personalization; GetResponse works for teams whose core need is email marketing with a lightweight funnel attached; and Builderall targets the most budget-constrained buyers still comparing tools on price alone.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving ClickFunnels means paying for funnel and checkout features you never touch, or trading its all-in-one stack for a landing-page specialist that can't run an email sequence or deliver a course. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing structure once contact or email limits are exceeded, platform breadth versus specialization, and review credibility on Software Advice, which we use as the single rating source across all nine so the numbers are directly comparable.

## TL;DR

1. **Systeme.io:** Best for bootstrapped creators who want a genuinely free all-in-one funnel, course, and email platform.
2. **Kartra:** Best for coaches and course sellers who want funnels, memberships, and a helpdesk in one login.
3. **GoHighLevel:** Best for agencies that want to white-label the platform and resell it as their own SaaS.
4. **Kajabi:** Best for course creators and coaches who need content delivery built around expertise, not checkout funnels.
5. **Leadpages:** Best for small businesses that want unlimited landing pages without ClickFunnels' full price tag.
6. **Unbounce:** Best for marketing teams that treat conversion-rate optimization as a dedicated discipline.
7. **Instapage:** Best for paid-media teams running high-volume campaigns that need post-click personalization.
8. **GetResponse:** Best for teams whose core need is email marketing with a lightweight sales funnel attached.
9. **Builderall:** Best for the most budget-constrained buyers still comparing tools purely on price.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool was evaluated using Software Advice review data (a Gartner Digital Markets property that uses the same review-verification methodology as Capterra and GetApp), vendor pricing pages, and company profiles on LinkedIn and Wikipedia. We deliberately use one rating source across all nine tools rather than mixing G2, Capterra, and Software Advice figures, because scores are not comparable across platforms with different review-verification and weighting rules. Every rating below is labeled as Software Advice and links to the profile it came from. Where a vendor self-reports a different score on its own site (Leadpages, for example, advertises 4.6/5 from G2), we cite the independently checked Software Advice figure instead.

The two factors that mattered most for this category: pricing structure once contact or email limits are exceeded, since that's the most cited reason ClickFunnels buyers look elsewhere, and platform breadth versus specialization, since some alternatives replace the entire funnel-checkout-course stack while others solve only the landing-page piece.

For the full process (every source we use, what disqualifies a tool, our conflict-of-interest handling, and our corrections policy) read [our research methodology and editorial policy](/research-methodology/).

## Why Look for a ClickFunnels Alternative?

The most cited reason buyers look past ClickFunnels is pricing escalation. Costs climb quickly once contact or email-send limits are exceeded, and Software Advice reviewers repeatedly describe it as funnel and checkout-centric rather than a true all-in-one CRM or course platform.

The second driver is fit by use case. Teams that need deep automation logic tend to look at GoHighLevel, teams focused on course delivery tend to look at Kajabi, and budget-first buyers tend to look at Systeme.io, each solving a piece of what ClickFunnels bundles into one (pricier) subscription.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best ClickFunnels alternative?

Systeme.io is strongest for budget-first buyers, given its free tier and 4.8/5 rating. Kartra is the closest all-in-one match for coaches and course sellers.

### Is there a free ClickFunnels alternative?

Yes. Systeme.io's Free plan runs at $0/mo permanently (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course), a genuine free tier rather than a time-limited trial.

### Which ClickFunnels alternative is cheapest?

Systeme.io is cheapest overall at $0/mo, then $17/mo paid. Leadpages' HTML Pub Starter runs $10/mo for publishing only, versus ClickFunnels' $97/mo Launch.

### Which ClickFunnels alternative is built for course creators?

Kajabi is purpose-built around course and membership content delivery. Kartra bundles course hosting alongside funnels and a helpdesk for a similar audience.

### Which ClickFunnels alternative is built for agencies?

GoHighLevel is the clearest agency pick, with white-label SaaS mode and rebillable sub-accounts that let agencies resell the platform under their own brand.

### Which ClickFunnels alternative has the highest rating?

Systeme.io holds the highest Software Advice rating in this roster at 4.8/5 across 2,128 reviews, the largest review sample of any tool compared here.

Yes. ClickFunnels offers a 14-day free trial followed by a 30-day money-back guarantee, based on its published pricing at clickfunnels.com/pricing.
