Comparing the top 8 best Serpstat alternatives in 2026 includes 1. Ahrefs, 2. Semrush, 3. SE Ranking, 4. Moz Pro, 5. Mangools, 6. SpyFu, 7. Seobility, 8. Ubersuggest.

Ahrefs and Semrush are the picks for agencies and SaaS teams that outgrew Serpstat's backlink and keyword index; SE Ranking and Moz Pro suit teams that want comparable feature depth with white-label reporting or a beginner-friendly interface; Mangools and SpyFu fit solo founders and PPC-leaning teams on a tighter budget; and Seobility and Ubersuggest are honest lateral moves on price, not database depth, better suited to teams whose real complaint about Serpstat was cost.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving Serpstat means paying more for a database that's barely bigger, or trading Serpstat's flat pricing for per-seat costs you didn't budget for. Each tool below was evaluated against Serpstat's 8.5B-keyword index on database depth, pricing transparency, free-trial availability, and what G2 and Capterra reviewers say about the switch.

## TL;DR

1. **Ahrefs:** Best for teams whose main complaint about Serpstat is backlink and keyword database depth.
2. **Semrush:** Best for teams that want SEO, PPC, content, and AI-visibility tracking bundled with a bigger index than Serpstat's.
3. **SE Ranking:** Best for agencies that want Serpstat-level pricing predictability plus native white-label reporting.
4. **Moz Pro:** Best for teams that want the Domain Authority metric and an easier learning curve than Serpstat's dashboard.
5. **Mangools:** Best for solo SaaS founders who want a simpler, cheaper tool than Serpstat's all-in-one suite.
6. **SpyFu:** Best for teams whose Serpstat complaint is specifically thin PPC and ad-history data.
7. **Seobility:** Best for teams whose real complaint about Serpstat is on-page audit depth, not backlink or keyword scale.
8. **Ubersuggest:** Best for early-stage teams that liked Serpstat's affordability but need something even cheaper.

### 1. Ahrefs

Best for: Teams whose main complaint about Serpstat is backlink and keyword database depth.

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) runs its own independent web crawler, billed as the largest after Google and Bing, which is the exact gap that pushes teams off Serpstat's smaller index. It's the direct upgrade when database size is the whole reason for switching.

**Tool Snapshot**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Web app |
| Free Trial | No paid-plan trial; Ahrefs Free tier available |
| Starting Price | [$29/mo](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) (Starter) |
| Rating | [4.5/5 (674 reviews, G2)](https://www.g2.com/products/ahrefs/reviews) |

**Why Over Serpstat**

Ahrefs' independently-crawled backlink index returns materially deeper link data than Serpstat's, which independent testing found surfaces only 55 to 65 percent of the backlinks Ahrefs reports across a 20-domain sample. Content and traffic-estimate tooling is also stronger.

- Independently-crawled backlink index, deeper than Serpstat's on third-party testing
- Stronger content and traffic-estimate tooling than Serpstat's PPC-lite suite
- Used by marketers at named Fortune 500 accounts including Adobe, IBM, and HubSpot (vendor-published)

**What Users Say**

**Love:** "Deep keyword database, endless filters"
A G2 reviewer notes Ahrefs "has a deep database of keyword and statistic plus an endless amount of filters." Source: [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/ahrefs/reviews).

- "There are cheaper tools, but this is the best I have found for keyword research," per another G2 reviewer.

**Complain:** "Materially pricier than Serpstat at every tier"
Ahrefs' Lite plan runs 2.6 times Serpstat's Individual tier, with no free trial on paid plans and a learning curve reviewers flag consistently.

- No free trial on any paid plan, unlike Serpstat's 7-day trial
- Onboarding/learning-curve complaints recur across G2 and Capterra reviews

**Fit Profile**

It's for agencies and SaaS teams that scaled past Serpstat's index and need the deepest backlink and keyword data available, regardless of price.

It's NOT for budget-first teams; Ahrefs costs more at every comparable tier and skips the free trial Serpstat offers.

**Our Read**

We'd send teams here first when backlink depth, not price, was the actual reason they were unhappy with Serpstat.

## Why Look for a Serpstat Alternative?

The most common reason teams leave Serpstat is database depth. Independent testing found Serpstat surfaces roughly 55 to 65 percent of the backlinks Ahrefs reports across a 20-domain sample, workable for lightweight competitive checks but not enough once link building or large-scale agency reporting becomes the primary use case. G2 reviewer consensus echoes the same complaint: Serpstat's backlink database completeness trails the market leaders.

The second driver is scale. Serpstat's own numbers (8.5B+ keywords, 230+ regional Google databases) are respectable for the price, but agencies managing dozens of client accounts or SaaS teams tracking a crowded competitive set often need a bigger index before the data becomes unreliable at the edges. Reviewers also note occasional interface lag on larger datasets, another symptom of the same scale ceiling.

Not every switch is really about database depth, though. Some Serpstat moves are about price (Ubersuggest, Mangools), audit focus (Seobility), or PPC intelligence (SpyFu), separate from the backlink and keyword database gap that drives the bigger-name upgrades.

Ahrefs is the strongest pick for backlink and keyword database depth. Semrush is the best all-in-one alternative for broader channel coverage.

### Is Semrush better than Serpstat?

Semrush covers more channels (SEO, PPC, content, AI-visibility) and reports a bigger index, but it costs noticeably more at every comparable tier.

SE Ranking's Core plan runs about double Serpstat's $50/mo Individual tier, though it adds a fuller native white-label suite and a longer trial.

Ubersuggest is the cheapest at $29/mo, undercutting Serpstat's $50/mo Individual tier, though its database is smaller than Serpstat's own.

Compared with Ahrefs and Semrush specifically, yes. Independent testing found Serpstat surfaces 55 to 65 percent of the backlinks Ahrefs reports.

### Which Serpstat alternative has the best backlink data?

Ahrefs' independently-crawled index is generally considered the deepest in this roster, with Semrush's blended index a close second.

### Is Ubersuggest a full Serpstat replacement?

No. Ubersuggest is cheaper, but it has the smallest keyword and backlink database in this roster, including versus Serpstat itself.
