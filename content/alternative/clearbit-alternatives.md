Comparing the top 9 best Clearbit alternatives in 2026 includes 1. Apollo.io, 2. ZoomInfo, 3. Cognism, 4. Lusha, 5. Clay, 6. 6sense, 7. Demandbase, 8. Warmly, 9. RB2B.

Apollo.io and Lusha fit self-serve teams that want a card-and-go signup instead of a sales call; ZoomInfo and Cognism suit enterprise and EMEA-focused teams ready to sign an annual contract for the deepest data; Clay targets technical RevOps teams that want to orchestrate many providers instead of relying on one; and 6sense, Demandbase, Warmly, and RB2B each solve a narrower job, from full ABM orchestration to simple free website-visitor identification.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving Clearbit means paying enterprise contract prices for a feature set a self-serve vendor already covers, or landing on a narrow point tool that can't handle bulk enrichment at all. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, review credibility, and how directly it answers the reason most buyers are shopping in the first place: Clearbit's 2023 acquisition by HubSpot and its now-unpublished pricing.

## TL;DR

1. **Apollo.io:** Best for teams that want a self-serve, all-in-one prospecting, enrichment, and sequencing platform.
2. **ZoomInfo:** Best for enterprise teams that need the deepest B2B contact database and can sign an annual contract.
3. **Cognism:** Best for EMEA-focused teams that need GDPR-compliant, mobile-verified contact data.
4. **Lusha:** Best for budget-conscious teams that want a low-commitment, credit-based self-serve start.
5. **Clay:** Best for technical RevOps teams that want to orchestrate 200+ data providers instead of one.
6. **6sense:** Best for teams that want firmographic and intent data folded into a full ABM platform.
7. **Demandbase:** Best for enterprise ABX teams that want intent, advertising, and sales intelligence combined.
8. **Warmly:** Best for teams that want website-visitor deanonymization paired with AI outreach agents.
9. **RB2B:** Best for teams that just want free-to-start, person-level website visitor identification.

## Why Look for a Clearbit Alternative?

The most cited reason buyers look past Clearbit today is the acquisition itself. HubSpot bought Clearbit in December 2023, and the tool now ships as "Clearbit by HubSpot," feeding HubSpot's Breeze Intelligence data layer; its own pricing page shows features only, with no listed tiers, directing shoppers to contact sales or HubSpot instead.

The second driver is audience fit. Clearbit built its brand on a developer-first, API-enrichment motion, and buyers who don't run HubSpot as their core CRM lose the tool's main integration value. That's the exact group now evaluating whether they're buying an independent tool or a bundled HubSpot add-on, and it's why standalone, API-first alternatives on this list get shortlisted first.
