Comparing the top 7 best 6sense alternatives in 2026 includes 1. Demandbase, 2. ZoomInfo, 3. Apollo.io, 4. Bombora, 5. Warmly, 6. AdRoll ABM, 7. Cognism.

Demandbase is the closest like-for-like replacement for enterprise ABM teams who want the same full-funnel orchestration 6sense offers; ZoomInfo fits teams that used 6sense mainly for account prioritization and need reachable contact data alongside intent; Apollo.io is the accessible pick for SMB and mid-market teams that need intent signals without a five-figure contract; Bombora suits teams that already own a GTM stack and just want intent data bolted on; Warmly is the fast-to-deploy option for warm-outbound and visitor identification; AdRoll ABM is built for lean teams that mainly need ad execution and retargeting; and Cognism is the strongest fit for sales teams whose motion runs through EMEA cold-calling.

Picking the wrong ABM platform after leaving 6sense costs more than the contract itself: it means paying for orchestration you don't use, missing reachable contacts on accounts you've correctly identified, or locking into another six-figure deal that doesn't fix the complaint that pushed you to switch. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, intent-data accuracy, contact-data quality, and how directly it addresses the specific reasons buyers leave 6sense: cost, contract terms, learning curve, and contact-data gaps.

## TL;DR

1. **Demandbase:** Best for enterprise ABM teams that want the closest like-for-like replacement for 6sense's full orchestration suite.
2. **ZoomInfo:** Best for teams that used 6sense for account prioritization and need contact-level accuracy alongside intent.
3. **Apollo.io:** Best for SMB and mid-market teams that need intent data and contacts without an enterprise budget.
4. **Bombora:** Best for teams that already own a MAP/CRM/ad stack and want a pure intent-data layer, not a full platform.
5. **Warmly:** Best for lean revenue teams that want fast visitor identification and warm-outbound alerts, not full orchestration.
6. **AdRoll ABM:** Best for lean marketing teams that mainly need ABM advertising and retargeting execution.
7. **Cognism:** Best for sales teams whose primary motion is EMEA cold-calling with GDPR-compliant mobile data.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Each tool was evaluated using indexed G2 review data (6sense's own product pages return HTTP 403 to automated fetch tools, so ratings are drawn from search-aggregated snippets of the live G2 pages, plus purchase-data aggregators like Vendr-style contract reports for pricing that vendors don't publish). Named-client claims were cross-checked against each vendor's own case-study pages where available.

The two criteria that mattered most for this category: pricing transparency (6sense's biggest cited complaint is contract opacity, so we flagged every price that isn't vendor-published as an estimate) and whether a tool actually fixes the specific gap driving the switch, since "inaccurate contact data" and "overkill for mid-market" are two very different reasons to leave, with two very different right answers.
