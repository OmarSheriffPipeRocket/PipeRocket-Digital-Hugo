Comparing the top 9 best Similarweb alternatives in 2026 includes 1. Semrush, 2. Ahrefs, 3. SE Ranking, 4. SpyFu, 5. Sistrix, 6. BuiltWith, 7. Owler, 8. Crayon, 9. Sensor Tower.

Semrush and Ahrefs suit teams that want the deepest all-in-one keyword and backlink data alongside traffic intelligence; SE Ranking and SpyFu fit budget-conscious teams that still want a full SEO or PPC competitive toolkit; Sistrix serves DACH-region SEO teams tracking long-running visibility trends; BuiltWith targets prospecting teams that need technology-stack data instead of traffic estimates; Owler and Crayon suit sales and product-marketing teams that need competitor news and battlecards rather than web analytics; and Sensor Tower fits teams whose real competitive-intelligence need is mobile-app performance.

Picking the wrong tool after leaving Similarweb means paying enterprise-tier prices for a feature set a cheaper vendor already covers, or trading Similarweb's traffic panel for a tool that never measures web traffic at all. Each tool below was evaluated on pricing transparency, review credibility, and how directly it answers the two most-cited reasons buyers look past Similarweb: its steep price jump past the Starter tier and inconsistent data accuracy for smaller sites.

## TL;DR

1. **Semrush:** Best for teams that want the deepest all-in-one SEO, content, and PPC suite.
2. **Ahrefs:** Best for teams whose top priority is backlink-index depth and link-building research.

7. **Owler:** Best for sales and BD teams that want crowdsourced competitor news and account research.
8. **Crayon:** Best for product-marketing and sales-enablement teams building competitor battlecards.
9. **Sensor Tower:** Best for teams whose Similarweb use case is mobile-app competitive intelligence.

## How We Chose These Tools?

Every price in this roundup was read off each vendor's own live pricing page on August 6, 2026, and every client name and testimonial was traced back to the vendor's own customers or case-studies page. Ratings come from each tool's G2 profile; where a G2 figure could not be re-read directly, it is labelled with its review count so you can judge the sample size yourself.

The two factors that mattered most for this category: pricing transparency, since Similarweb's jump from a roughly $125-199/mo Starter plan to a reported $14,000/yr Team tier is the most cited reason buyers look elsewhere, and data-depth fit, since several alternatives here solve a narrower job (technology detection, mobile intelligence, sales battlecards) rather than a like-for-like traffic-estimation swap.

## Why Look for a Similarweb Alternative?

The most cited reason buyers look past Similarweb is its pricing structure. A roughly $125-199/mo Starter plan jumps to a reported $14,000/yr Team tier, with Business and Enterprise packages reported to start around $16,000+/yr and quoted only through sales, a steep climb for teams that just want ongoing competitive-traffic tracking.

The second driver is data accuracy for smaller sites. Reviewers consistently note that Similarweb's traffic estimates get less reliable for domains under roughly 100,000 monthly visits, and several of its most useful insights sit behind the higher-priced tiers rather than the entry plan. Its 7-day free trial also requires a credit card and caps usage at 15 actions per day, a real limitation for teams that want to test the data before committing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Similarweb alternative?

Semrush is the strongest all-in-one pick for teams that want SEO, content, and PPC data together. Ahrefs is the closer match on backlink-index depth alone.

### Is there a cheaper Similarweb alternative?

Yes. SE Ranking, Ahrefs, and SpyFu all start well below Similarweb's roughly $125-199/mo Starter plan, and Ahrefs and Owler both offer genuine free tiers.

### Which Similarweb alternative is best for PPC competitor research?

SpyFu is built specifically around competitor ad history and paid-keyword intelligence, an area where it self-positions ahead of both Semrush and Ahrefs.

### Which alternative is best for European or DACH-region SEO?

Sistrix, with its proprietary Visibility Index and strong precision in German-speaking markets, though its usefulness narrows meaningfully outside that region.

### Does any alternative replace Similarweb's mobile-app intelligence?

Sensor Tower is the closest match, combining its own app-store data with the acquired data.ai/App Annie dataset, though its pricing is enterprise-only.

### Which tools here aren't traffic-intelligence tools at all?

BuiltWith detects technology stacks, Owler tracks crowdsourced company news, and Crayon builds sales battlecards; none measure web traffic the way Similarweb does.

### Is Crayon a good self-serve option?

No. Crayon has no public pricing and no free trial; every plan requires a sales conversation before you see a price.
