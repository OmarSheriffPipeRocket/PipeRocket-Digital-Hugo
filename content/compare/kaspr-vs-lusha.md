## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both tools solve the same core problem (reveal a contact's email and phone number and route it into a workflow), and each side wins rows the other doesn't. The honest differences are review consistency, client roster, integration depth, and how the credit math works out at scale.

| Axis | Kaspr | Lusha |
|---|---|---|
| **Pricing model** | Unlimited email credits from $49/mo; phone/direct-email credits capped by tier | Per-reveal credit model from $49.90/mo (1 credit/email, 10 credits/phone) |
| **Best-fit buyer** | LinkedIn-first SDRs and small European/SMB teams on a budget | Teams wanting a broader, enterprise-proven contact database |
| **Client roster** | SMB / mid-market European (The Startup Effect, SalesLabs, Cloud Direct, Craft AI) | Enterprise logos (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Salesforce, Spotify, Snowflake) |
| **Review consistency** | Wide spread across platforms (G2 4.4 vs. Trustpilot 1.5, reported); Capterra sample is only 3 reviews | Consistent across platforms (G2 4.3/~1,492 reviews; Capterra 4.0/398 reviews) |
| **Integrations** | Native Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Lemlist, Brevo, Aircall, Ringover; Outreach/Salesloft unconfirmed | Native Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, plus confirmed native Outreach and Salesloft |
| **Compliance certification** | GDPR and CCPA badges only, per its own site | GDPR, CCPA, plus SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 31700 certifications |
| **Parent-company backing** | Acquired by Cognism in 2022; benefits from a larger group's data/compliance infrastructure | Independent company, roughly 300 employees |
| **Cost at scale for phone reveals** | Business tier ($79/mo) includes 200 phone credits/mo outright | Phone reveals cost 10 credits each (up from 5), so cost climbs faster with volume |
