short_answer:
  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    Otterly.AI and AthenaHQ are both AI visibility (AEO) trackers that monitor
    how a brand shows up in AI answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google
    AI Overviews. The honest split is entry cost versus engine breadth and
    enterprise depth. Otterly.AI is the cheaper, longer-running option: it
    launched in 2024, starts at $29/mo with a no-card 7-day trial, and covers
    four core engines out of the gate. AthenaHQ is the younger, broader
    platform: it launched out of Y Combinator's Winter 2025 batch, starts at
    $295/mo for full paid access, but tracks 9 AI models including Grok and
    adds enterprise features (audit logs, multi-region, SOC 2 Type 2, a
    brand-claim discrepancy detector) that Otterly.AI does not publish. Note
    that SSO alone is not the dividing line: Otterly.AI does list Single
    Sign-on on its own custom Enterprise tier. Both vendors sit on thin G2
    review bases (roughly 30 to 50 reviews each), and G2's pages return a
    bot-blocked response on direct fetch, so the rating figures below are
    corroborated through search-indexed G2 listings and vendor-displayed
    badges rather than a first-hand page render.
