    UpLead and Apollo.io both sell into the same B2B prospecting workflow, but
    they are built for different jobs. UpLead is a data and enrichment tool: a
    contact database with real-time email verification at the point of export

    split is about scope and price predictability; on raw data quality the two
    are closely matched.

faqs:
  - q: "What is the difference between UpLead and Apollo.io?"
    a: >-
      UpLead is a B2B contact database and enrichment tool built around
      real-time email verification and a 95%+ accuracy guarantee with credit
      refunds; it does not include sequencing or dialing. Apollo.io is a
      full prospecting and engagement platform that bundles a contact
      database with multi-step sequences, a built-in dialer, an AI
      assistant, and a lightweight CRM. UpLead is priced on flat monthly
      credits; Apollo.io is priced per seat with a free tier and a credit
      system layered on top.
  - q: "Is UpLead more accurate than Apollo.io?"
    a: >-
      UpLead backs its data with an explicit 95%+ accuracy guarantee and
      automatic credit refunds on bad contacts. Apollo.io does not publish
      an equivalent formal guarantee, and G2 reviewers repeatedly cite
      bounce-rate and mobile-number accuracy complaints, with community
      reports putting Apollo's unfiltered email accuracy around 65 to 70%
      (80 to 91% when filtered to verified-only). This favors UpLead on
      accuracy specifically, though Apollo's much larger review pool means
      it has more total feedback to weigh, positive and negative.
  - q: "Which is cheaper, UpLead or Apollo.io?"
    a: >-
      On paper, Apollo.io's entry tier is cheaper for a single seat (Basic
      $49/user/mo annual versus UpLead Essentials at $74/mo annual), and
      Apollo also offers a genuine free tier that UpLead does not. But
      Apollo's credit system spans exports, mobile unlocks, and sequences,
      and several independent pricing breakdowns report real-world spend
      commonly landing at $150 to $400/user/mo once overages hit. UpLead's
      flat credit allotments with a fixed $0.60 overage rate are generally
      more predictable for a lean list-building workflow.
  - q: "Does UpLead include a dialer or email sequencing?"
    a: >-
      No. UpLead is a data and enrichment tool only. It has no built-in
      dialer, call recording, or multi-step sequencing engine. Teams that
      want to prospect, verify, and export from UpLead need a separate
      outreach or CRM tool to actually run sequences and calls. Apollo.io
      includes both a built-in dialer and multi-step sequencing natively.
  - q: "Does Apollo.io have a real free plan?"
    a: >-
      Yes. Apollo.io's free tier grants 900 credits per seat per year
      (issued monthly), a limited AI assistant, two sequences, and 250 daily
      emails, and it does not expire. UpLead's free option is a 7-day trial
      with 5 credits and no ongoing free tier once the trial ends.
  - q: "Which has more social proof, UpLead or Apollo.io?"
    a: >-
      Apollo.io, by a wide margin. Apollo reports roughly 9,344 G2 reviews
      and about 383 Capterra reviews, versus UpLead's roughly 700 to 824 G2
      reviews and 76 Capterra reviews. UpLead's Capterra sample in
      particular is low-N and should be read as directional rather than
      statistically robust. Confirm both counts live before citing them, as

      the time of research.

## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both tools surface B2B contacts, and each side wins rows the other does not. The honest differences are scope, data-accuracy guarantees, and how predictable the bill is.

| Axis | UpLead | Apollo.io |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Data and enrichment only; no sequencing or dialer | All-in-one: data, sequences, dialer, AI assistant, lightweight CRM |
| **Database size** | ~200M+ contacts (self-reported) | ~240M to 275M+ contacts (source-dependent) |
| **Accuracy guarantee** | ✓ Explicit 95%+ guarantee with automatic credit refunds | No formal guarantee; reviewers cite bounce-rate and mobile-accuracy complaints |
| **Pricing predictability** | Flat monthly credits, fixed $0.60 overage rate | Per-seat plus credit system across exports, mobile, and sequences; real spend often exceeds sticker price |
| **Free plan** | 7-day trial only, not ongoing | Perpetual free tier, 900 credits/seat/year |
| **Team access** | Single-user on entry tiers; multi-seat requires custom Professional plan | Per-seat pricing scales to any team size from Basic upward |
| **Interface** | Simpler, focused on prospecting/list-building | Broader feature surface; some reviewers describe it as complex for new users |
| **Public proof** | 4.7 G2 (~700 to 824 reviews); 4.6 Capterra (76, low-N) | 4.7 G2 (~9,344 reviews); 4.5 Capterra (~383) |
