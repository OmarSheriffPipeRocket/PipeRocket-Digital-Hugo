## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both tools do the core job well and sit in the same high-satisfaction band at nearly the same price. The real differences are at the edges, and each side wins rows the other does not.

| Axis | Surfer SEO | Frase |
|---|---|---|
| **SERP analysis** | Deep: word count, headings, NLP terms, structure, backlinks, traffic, images (500+ web signals) | Scans top ~20 results for topics, outlines, topic scores, content gaps; less granular per reviews |
| **Scoring model** | Rank-correlation + NLP; over-optimization risk to manage | Topic/term coverage + NLP; reviewers note TF-IDF-style term weighting can miss semantic depth |
| **AI writing** | Included; refresh cadence scales with tier | Included; reviewers note output is stiff/formal with weak intros/closings and needs polish |
| **Briefs** | Available (outline/brief building) | A core strength: question research, auto outlines, briefs |
| **GEO / AI visibility** | AI SEO guidelines; brand knowledge for AI | Built-in AI Search Tracking across ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini (by tier) |
| **Monitoring** | Rank-drop detection, cannibalization report (higher tiers), internal linking | Content Guard page monitoring (3/15/50 by tier), site audits |
| **GSC integration** | Google Search Console among integrations | No native GSC integration per multiple reviews; a trigger for 100+ page teams to look elsewhere |
| **Entry / top price** | $49/mo (Discovery, yearly) to $299/mo (Peace of Mind) | $49/mo ($39 annual, Starter) to $299/mo ($239 annual, Scale) |
| **Public proof** | 4.8 G2 (~544 reviews); larger G2 review pool | 4.8 G2 (~297 reviews); close on Capterra volume |
