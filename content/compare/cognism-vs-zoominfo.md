short_answer:
  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    Cognism and ZoomInfo are both B2B sales intelligence platforms built around
    a contact database, but they specialize in different territory. Cognism is
    a single-product sales intelligence tool built around phone-verified
    mobile data ("Diamond Data") and GDPR/CCPA-first compliance tooling, with
    its strongest coverage in the UK and EMEA. ZoomInfo is a much larger,
    multi-product GTM data platform (Sales, Marketing, Talent, Operations)
    with a bigger US-centric database, intent data, and an AI copilot, sold
    to a much wider customer base. Neither publishes public pricing; both are
    quote-only via demo.
  callouts:
    - label: "Choose Cognism"
      title: "EMEA phone data and compliance-first tooling, single-product focus"
      body: >-
        If your ICP sells into the **UK or EMEA**, you need **phone-verified
        mobile numbers** and built-in GDPR/CCPA (DNC/TPS) screening, and you
        want a simpler, single-product tool rather than a large multi-line
        suite, Cognism is the more specialized pick (reported ~$22,500/yr
        estimate for a 5-seat plan, quote-only).
    - label: "Choose ZoomInfo"
      title: "Larger US-centric database, intent data, and a multi-product GTM suite"
      body: >-
        If you need **broader US contact depth**, intent signals, and a
        platform that spans Sales, Marketing, Talent, and Operations with
        deep native integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics,
        Salesloft, Outreach, Marketo), ZoomInfo's larger scale is the stronger
        fit (reported ~$37,500/yr estimate for a 5-seat Advanced plan,
        quote-only).

## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both platforms cover the sales-intelligence core (contact data, filtering, CRM enrichment) and each side wins rows the other does not. The honest differences are geographic specialization, product breadth, and reported cost.

| Axis | Cognism | ZoomInfo |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Single product: sales intelligence with phone-verified data and compliance tooling | Multi-product GTM platform: Sales, Marketing, Talent, Operations |
| **Geographic strength** | UK and EMEA phone verification is the stated core specialization | US-centric contact depth; covers more industries and geographies more evenly overall |
| **Scale** | ~559 employees; ~6,400 customers (reported estimate) | ~3,500 employees; 35,000+ customers, over half the Fortune 500 |
| **Compliance** | GDPR/CCPA-first (DNC/TPS screening, ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II) built around European data law | Broader compliance posture, not positioned as an EMEA-first specialist |
| **Intent data** | Included as part of the platform | Positioned as a core strength, sold as an add-on module |
| **Integrations** | Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive confirmed; broader list not itemized publicly | Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, Salesloft, Outreach, Marketo, plus an app marketplace and MCP server |
| **Pricing** | Quote-only; reported ~$22,500/yr estimate for a 5-seat plan | Quote-only; reported ~$37,495/yr estimate for a comparable 5-seat plan |
| **Public proof** | ~4.6/5 G2, 500+ reviews (reported, verify live) | ~4.5/5 G2, ~12,882 reviews across product family (reported, verify live) |
