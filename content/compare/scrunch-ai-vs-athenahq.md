    Scrunch AI and AthenaHQ are both platforms built to track and improve how
    brands show up in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other AI
    answer engines. The honest split runs along maturity versus independence.

    it was acquired by Sitecore in June 2026 in a deal Bloomberg and Adweek

    carries a higher G2 rating, edges Scrunch on model coverage (9 versus 8,
    with Grok live on AthenaHQ and listed as "coming soon" by Scrunch), and
    leans harder into an automated "fix it for you" agent rather than a
    monitoring-only dashboard. Headline subscription prices are close enough
    to be a wash once billing terms are matched; the real price difference is
    trial access. Neither company has a review base large enough to be
    statistically decisive on its own as of July 2026.
