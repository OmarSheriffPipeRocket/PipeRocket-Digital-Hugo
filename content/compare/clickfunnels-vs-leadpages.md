    a: >-
      Neither is better in the abstract; they solve different problems.

      email marketing bundled into one subscription instead of stitching
      together separate tools. Leadpages is the stronger pick if you already
      have those tools elsewhere and want deeper, dedicated CRO features, or
      if you only need a cheap page-publishing tool without any conversion
      optimization at all.
