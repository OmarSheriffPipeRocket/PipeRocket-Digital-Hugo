## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both tools do the core job well (help you optimize Google Ads accounts through automation or guided suggestions). The honest differences are at the edges, and each side wins some rows.

| Axis | Optmyzr | Opteo |
|---|---|---|
| **Optimization model** | Rule Engine: custom if/then automation you build and maintain | Recommendation engine: guided, prioritized suggestions you review and apply |
| **Automation style** | Active, rule-based; can run scheduled automations | Passive; manual review-and-apply (no auto-execution) |
| **Platform coverage** | Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, LinkedIn Ads | Google Ads only (Microsoft confirmed not on roadmap) |
| **Account scale** | Built for large portfolios (50+ accounts) | Best under roughly 10 to 25 accounts |
| **Bid / budget** | Cross-platform budget management, portfolio bidding | Budget pacing = monitoring and alerts only (no daily auto-adjust) |
| **Entry price** | ~$208+/mo (Essentials, billed annually) | $129/mo (Basic) |
| **Learning curve** | Steep; 4 to 6 weeks to proficiency commonly reported | Fast and intuitive; smaller feature set |
| **Public proof** | 4.7 G2 (128) and 4.7 Capterra (184); deeper review pool | 4.9 Capterra (114) but 4.5 G2 on 41 reviews |
