  intro: >-
    Crazy Egg and Hotjar are both long-running heatmap and session-recording
    tools that grew into broader behavior-analytics suites, but they now sit
    on opposite sides of an ownership divide. Crazy Egg, founded in 2006 by
    Hiten Shah and Neil Patel, remains an independently operated, bootstrapped
    company with a fully itemized self-serve pricing page across four tiers
    ($29 to $599/mo) and native unlimited A/B testing and error tracking built
    into the same product. Hotjar, founded in 2014, was acquired by
    Contentsquare in September 2021 and then fully merged into Contentsquare
    Group on 1 July 2025, so it no longer operates as a standalone company;
    its dedicated pricing page now 308-redirects into Contentsquare's general
    pricing, and reviewers cite post-merger price increases and confusing
    tiers as a recurring complaint. Hotjar counters with real scale
    advantages: a claimed 1,306,323 websites versus Crazy Egg's 449,000+, a
    larger and more internationally recognizable client roster, and a
    materially bigger, higher-scoring review base on Capterra (543 reviews at
    4.6/5 versus Crazy Egg's 86 at 4.4/5). Both tools now offer a free plan,
    though Hotjar's includes capped heatmaps and session recordings while
    Crazy Egg's free plan covers web analytics, instant heatmaps, conversion
    analytics, and surveys without session recordings. Both public G2 ratings
    come from search-indexed snapshots rather than a live confirmed fetch, so
    treat those two figures as directional only.
