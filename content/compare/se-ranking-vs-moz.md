  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    SE Ranking and Moz Pro are both all-in-one SEO platforms, but they were
    built two decades apart and it shows in what each one optimizes for. Moz
    launched in 2004 as SEOmoz and invented Domain Authority, the metric that
    became an industry-wide shorthand for link-based site strength; it still
    ships one of the category's largest link indexes (roughly 44-45.5
    trillion links) and the free MozBar extension with 900,000+ Chrome users.
    SE Ranking launched later (2013 per its own about page, though a
    third-party source cites 2012) as a bootstrapped, budget-priced
    alternative that bundles rank tracking, site audits, PPC research,
    content tools, white-label agency reporting, and a dedicated AI Search
    Toolkit for tracking visibility inside AI Overviews and chat answers. Its
    headline price is higher than Moz's ($103.20/mo for Core versus $79/mo for
    Moz's comparable Standard tier, both annual), but it bundles materially
    more capacity per dollar: 2,000 daily-refreshed keywords and 10 projects
    against Moz Standard's 10 campaigns and roughly 1,500 keywords (a
    third-party figure that other trackers put as low as 300), plus PPC,
    content, and AI-search tooling Moz does not offer at any tier. Neither vendor's
    pricing page nor G2 review page could be fetched directly for this
    research pass (Moz blocks bots outright; both G2 pages return HTTP 403),
    so several figures below are flagged as cross-verified via search
    snapshots or third-party aggregators rather than a first-hand fetch.

faqs:
  - q: "What is the difference between SE Ranking and Moz Pro?"
    a: >-
      SE Ranking is a bootstrapped, budget-priced all-in-one platform that
      bundles rank tracking, site audits, PPC research, content tools,
      white-label agency reporting, and a dedicated AI Search Toolkit for
      tracking visibility in AI Overviews and chat answers. Moz Pro is the

      comparable tier, has no bundled PPC tooling, and has not confirmed
      shipping AI-search visibility tracking for 2026.

    a: >-
      Neither is better in the abstract. SE Ranking is the stronger pick if
      you want more tracked keywords and bundled PPC/content tools per

      tracking. Moz Pro is the stronger pick if you specifically need Domain
      Authority as a client-facing credibility metric, want access to one of
      the largest link indexes in the category, or value MozBar's free
      browser utility and Moz's two-decade brand history. The right answer
      depends on whether Domain Authority matters to your stakeholders and
      how much bundled tooling you need beyond core SEO.

## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both platforms handle the core SEO workflow competently. The real differences are bundled adjacent tooling, authority-metric recognition, and how up to date each vendor is on AI-search tracking, and each side wins rows the other does not.
