## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both tools do the core job (identifying anonymous website visitors at the company level), and each side wins some rows here, so this is not a case where one tool clearly beats the other on every axis.

| Axis | Leadfeeder | Albacross |
|---|---|---|
| **Review track record** | Larger sample (~860 G2 reviews) at a 4.3 average | Higher average (4.6) but on a much smaller sample (~120), less statistical weight |
| **Entry pricing** | Free Lite tier, then 79 EUR/mo paid | No free tier; 59 EUR/mo paid entry, 14-day trial |
| **Seat model** | Unlimited users on every paid plan | Gated by email/phone credits, not seats |
| **Outreach automation** | Not part of the core product | AI email/LinkedIn sequencing included at every tier |
| **Ad retargeting** | Not evident in listed integrations | Native LinkedIn/Google/Facebook Ads integration |
| **CRM depth on lower tiers** | HubSpot, Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Pipedrive, Zoho available broadly | Salesforce bi-directional sync reserved for top tier only |
| **Cancellation terms** | Reported 30-day notice on annual plans, reviewers note inflexibility | Not flagged in this research pass; verify directly |
| **Regional coverage** | Broader CRM/integration set globally | Reviewers flag weak North American support |
