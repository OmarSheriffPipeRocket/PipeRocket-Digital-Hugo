    SISTRIX and Semrush are both SEO platforms built around tracking organic
    search visibility, but they come from very different starting points.
    SISTRIX was founded in 2004 in Bonn, Germany, and built its reputation on
    the proprietary Visibility Index, a composite score that's become a de
    facto benchmark in DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) and wider European
    SEO circles. Semrush was founded in 2008 in Moscow, later moved its HQ to
    Boston, and grew into a much broader all-in-one marketing suite covering
    SEO, PPC, content, social, and (as of its 2026 tiers) AI-visibility
    monitoring, before being acquired outright by Adobe in April 2026 for
    roughly $1.9B. SISTRIX starts at EUR119/mo and stays the narrower tool: it
    now ships its own AI-visibility suite and an influencer/creator database,
    but still has no dedicated PPC or ad-intelligence module.
    Semrush starts at roughly $117/mo (annual) and covers far more ground, but
    it also carries fresh ownership-transition risk that SISTRIX, as an
    independently owned company, does not. Both vendors' public facts include
    real gaps and figures that vary by source, flagged plainly below rather
    than smoothed over.
