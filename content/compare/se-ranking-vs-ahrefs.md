  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    SE Ranking and Ahrefs both cover the SEO core: rank tracking, backlink
    analysis, site audits, and keyword research. The honest split is depth
    versus value. Ahrefs holds a materially larger backlink and keyword index
    and a globally recognized client roster, historically the tool agencies
    reach for when data depth and crawl freshness matter most. SE Ranking
    covers close to the same feature set at a lower entry price, bundles a
    genuine no-card 14-day free trial, and includes more tracked keywords and
    seats per dollar at the entry tier. One is the deeper, pricier data
    engine; the other is the budget-friendlier all-in-one with fewer strings
    attached at signup.

## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both platforms cover the SEO core competently. The real differences are index depth, seat and add-on economics, native reporting, and how each vendor prices AI visibility, and each side wins rows the other does not.

| Axis | SE Ranking | Ahrefs |
|---|---|---|
| **Backlink index depth** | ~2.7 to 3 trillion backlinks reported (vendor-published, unverified) | ~35 trillion backlinks and 28.7B+ keywords reported (vendor-published) |
| **Free trial** | Genuine 14-day, no credit card required, on all plans | None on paid plans; lowest-risk test is the $29/mo Starter tier |
| **Entry price** | $103.20/mo annual (Core): 2,000 tracked keywords | ~$108/mo annual (Lite): 750 tracked keywords |
| **Seats & collaboration** | Growth tier bundles 3 seats plus guest links at $223.20/mo | Extra seats cost $40 to $80/mo on top of plan price (verify live) |

| **AI/GEO visibility** | Bundled at entry tier; AI Search add-on ~$71/mo for expanded prompts | Brand Radar positioned as a more mature, dedicated AI-visibility product, from ~$199/mo (directional signal, not confirmed feature parity) |
| **Brand recognition** | Public references skew SMB/agency scale (Yamaha, WiseRIT); "40,000+ agencies" is an aggregate vendor claim | Homepage logo wall features globally recognized brands (eBay, IBM, LinkedIn, Shopify, HubSpot); "44% of Fortune 500" is a vendor-stated, unverified claim |

Both tools show strong ratings, but the samples differ in size and reliability. SE Ranking's G2 score edges higher (4.8 versus 4.5), though both G2 figures were sourced via search-result snippets because G2 blocks automated fetches, so confirm counts live before relying on them. The two tie at 4.7/5 on Capterra, but Ahrefs' figure rests on nearly double the review volume (585 versus 297, both confirmed live 2026-07-24), giving it more statistical depth at that rating level. SE Ranking's TrustRadius score (9.4/10) is based on just 39 reviews, a low-N sample flagged as non-statistical rather than a reliable signal. Backlink and keyword index sizes for both tools are vendor- or aggregator-sourced marketing figures, presented as directional rather than audited. The honest read is that both are capable platforms, and the choice tracks whether you need backlink depth and brand-name proof points or entry-tier value and a lower-risk trial, not which tool is "better."
