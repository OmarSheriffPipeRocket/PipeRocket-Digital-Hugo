short_answer:
  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    Mangools and Ubersuggest both sell themselves as affordable, beginner-friendly
    alternatives to the big enterprise SEO suites, and both start their entry
    tier around $29 a month. Past the entry price the two tools split into
    different bets. Mangools is a bootstrapped, five-tool organic SEO suite
    (keyword research, SERP analysis, rank tracking, backlink analysis, domain
    metrics) that holds the higher rating on every review platform checked but
    has no crawl-based technical audit and no AI content tools. Ubersuggest,
    owned and rebuilt by Neil Patel's NP Digital since 2017, packs in a full
    crawl-based site audit (up to 10,000 pages), an AI content ideation and
    writing assistant, native Google Analytics and WordPress integrations, and
    a one-time lifetime pricing option, but sits consistently lower on Capterra,
    G2, and Trustpilot and has drawn reviewer complaints about an upsell-heavy
    experience. The honest split is depth-of-ratings vs breadth-of-features.
