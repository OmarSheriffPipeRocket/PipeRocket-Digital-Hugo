short_answer:
  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    Cognism and Apollo.io both sell B2B contact and company data with outbound
    tooling on top, and both target sales and RevOps teams running outbound
    programs. The honest split comes down to data verification method,
    geography, and pricing model. Cognism is a data-first platform built
    around Diamond Data, human-operator-verified mobile numbers, with a strong
    reputation for EU, UK, and DACH coverage and GDPR compliance posture, sold
    entirely through a quote-gated, demo-required sales process with no
    published pricing. Apollo.io is an all-in-one, self-serve prospecting and
    engagement platform (data, sequences, dialer, and conversation
    intelligence) with transparent per-seat pricing and a free tier, skewing
    toward North America tech and SaaS ICPs.
  callouts:
    - label: "Choose Cognism"
      title: "Human-verified mobile data and EU/GDPR strength"
      body: >-
        If your outbound is UK, EU, or DACH-heavy and connect rate on mobile
        numbers matters more than anything else, Cognism's **Diamond Data**
        (human-operator-verified numbers, reported 2 to 3x higher connect
        rates) and its GDPR compliance posture are the more defensible pick,
        even though pricing requires a **demo and quote** rather than a
        published price list.
    - label: "Choose Apollo.io"
      title: "Self-serve, all-in-one, and transparently priced"
      body: >-

        data plus sequencing, dialing, and conversation intelligence bundled
        into **one tool**, and your ICP is North America-heavy tech or SaaS,
        Apollo.io is the more accessible and self-serve option.

faqs:
  - q: "What is the difference between Cognism and Apollo.io?"
    a: >-
      Cognism is a data-first sales intelligence platform built around
      Diamond Data, human-operator-verified mobile numbers, with strong
      EU/UK/DACH coverage and a GDPR-focused compliance posture, sold entirely
      through a demo-gated, quote-based sales process. Apollo.io is an
      all-in-one, self-serve prospecting and engagement platform bundling
      data, sequencing, dialing, and conversation intelligence with
      transparent per-seat pricing and a free tier. Cognism specializes in
      verified data quality and EU compliance; Apollo bundles more of the
      outreach workflow into one tool at a published, lower entry price.
  - q: "Is Cognism better than Apollo.io?"
    a: >-
      Neither is better in the abstract. Cognism is better for teams running
      EU, UK, or DACH-heavy outbound where mobile connect rate and GDPR
      compliance posture matter most, and who are comfortable with a
      quote-based sales process. Apollo.io is better for North America-heavy
      tech and SaaS teams that want a self-serve, transparently priced,
      all-in-one tool with a free tier to start. The right pick depends on
      target geography, budget process, and how much bundled engagement
      tooling you need, not overall quality.

## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both tools do the core job (surface B2B contacts and help you reach them), and each side wins rows the other does not. The honest differences are verification method, geography, and pricing model.

| Axis | Cognism | Apollo.io |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Data-first platform: contacts, company data, Bombora intent add-on | All-in-one: data, sequences, dialer, and conversation intelligence in one tool |
| **Data verification** | Diamond Data: human-operator-dialed and verified mobile numbers, reported 2 to 3x higher connect rates | Sourced database; reported weaker EMEA/DACH mobile-number verification than Cognism |
| **Geography skew** | Strongest for EU, UK, and DACH; reported weaker North America and APAC depth | Strongest for US tech/SaaS; reported weaker EMEA/DACH accuracy |
| **Compliance posture** | Heavily marketed GDPR compliance; frequently cited by EU enterprise buyers as the deciding factor | Compliance less frequently highlighted as a differentiator |
| **Pricing model** | Quote-gated; no published prices; reported platform fee ~$15,000 to $25,000/yr plus per-seat costs (unverified estimates) | Transparent per-seat tiers; free tier; credit-based add-ons criticized for hidden/escalating costs |
| **Access** | Demo required for any pricing or trial | Functional free plan; self-serve signup |
| **Public proof** | 4.5/5 G2 (~1,201 reviews; a separate G2 page cites 4.6/500+, discrepancy unresolved) | 4.7/5 G2 (~9,344 to 9,645 reviews depending on snapshot date) |
