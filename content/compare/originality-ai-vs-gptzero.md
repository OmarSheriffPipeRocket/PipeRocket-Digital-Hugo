Neither G2 figure below should be treated as settled. G2 returned a 403 to direct fetch for both products, so the numbers here come from indexed snapshots, and those snapshots disagree with each other. GPTZero shows up consistently at 4.3/5 from roughly 98 to 101 reviews. Originality.ai appears variously at 4.3/5 from 108 reviews on one G2 comparison page and 4.6/5 from 136 reviews elsewhere, with a total review count as high as 229 on another snapshot. Anyone using G2 as a tiebreaker should open both profiles and read the live numbers rather than rely on these.

Capterra is the one review source we could verify directly, and both samples are too thin to carry much weight: Originality.ai holds a clean 5.0/5 from just 7 reviews, while GPTZero sits at 3.0/5 from only 2 reviews, both of which flag accuracy swings and false positives on human content. GPTZero's self-announced "#1 Best AI Software Product of 2025" G2 award is the vendor's own press release rather than an independently re-verified leaderboard snapshot. Neither vendor's self-reported detection-accuracy percentage has been independently audited in this comparison, and both publish multiple different figures across different models and benchmarks. The honest read is that both tools carry real accuracy complaints from actual users, and the choice comes down to workflow fit (publisher and agency bulk QA versus academic and LMS deployment with a free-to-start trial) more than which score is technically higher.
