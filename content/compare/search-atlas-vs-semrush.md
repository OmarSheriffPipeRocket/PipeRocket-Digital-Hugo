## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both platforms handle the core SEO workflow competently. The real differences are execution model, data depth, and company transparency, and each side wins rows the other does not.

| Axis | Search Atlas | Semrush |
|---|---|---|
| **Execution model** | Autonomous OTTO agent deploys fixes directly to the live site | Reports and recommends; no autonomous execution layer |
| **Entry pricing** | $99/mo, but credit-based system has reported scaling friction | $139/mo ($117.33/mo annual), higher entry cost but no credit system |
| **PPC/advertising intelligence** | No dedicated module (Smart Ads is automation, not competitive intel) | Dedicated Advertising toolkit, a clear structural differentiator |
| **AI/LLM visibility tracking** | Bundled from Growth tier ($199/mo) up | Bundled from base SEO tier ($139/mo) since the 2026 restructure |

| **Digital PR / press syndication** | Built in via the 2024 Signal Genesys acquisition | No direct equivalent |
| **Company transparency** | Founding year and headcount vary across sources (2019 agency vs. 2021-22 platform; 75 vs. ~155 employees) | Public company (NYSE: SEMR), founded 2008, audited financials |
| **Named customer proof** | Logo strip (Hyundai, P&G, Shutterfly, AutoTrader); case studies lean on smaller/anonymized businesses | Named, attributed case studies (Samsung, Square, AutoScout24) |
