short_answer:
  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    Seamless.AI and ZoomInfo both sell B2B contact data, but they solve
    different problems at different price points. Seamless.AI is a real-time,
    credit-based contact finder (Chrome extension plus web app) built for a
    rep to look up one person's email or phone number on the spot, priced
    accessibly enough for individual reps and small teams. ZoomInfo is a full
    go-to-market intelligence platform (SalesOS, MarketingOS, OperationsOS)
    combining a much larger database with intent data, org charts, and
    workflow tooling, sold through a sales-led annual contract aimed at
    mid-market and enterprise revenue teams.
  callouts:
    - label: "Choose Seamless.AI"
      title: "Fast, affordable single-contact lookups for individual reps"
      body: >-
        If you want a **self-serve tool** for finding one person's direct
        contact info right now, work solo or on a small team, and want the
        lowest entry price (reported street pricing around **$79 to $150 per
        user per month** on paid tiers), Seamless.AI is the more accessible
        pick. Its free plan includes 50 lifetime credits.
    - label: "Choose ZoomInfo"
      title: "Full GTM data platform with intent data and org charts"
      body: >-
        If you need **intent data**, detailed org charts, and a multi-product
        suite that spans sales, marketing, and operations, and you have
        budget for a **sales-led annual contract** (realistic mid-market spend
        commonly runs **$25,000 to $50,000 a year**), ZoomInfo is the stronger
        fit for a full revenue team.

## Strengths & tradeoffs

Both tools do the core job (surface a contact's details), and each side wins rows the other does not. The honest differences are scope, data depth, and price.

| Axis | Seamless.AI | ZoomInfo |
|---|---|---|
| **Core model** | Real-time, credit-based contact finder | Full GTM data platform (Sales/Marketing/Operations OS) |
| **Pricing floor** | Reported ~$79 to $150/user/mo, quote-based | Reported ~$14,995+/yr entry, sales-led quote |
| **Intent data** | None; not a core capability | Core differentiator (Scoops, buyer intent signals) |
| **Org charts / account mapping** | Limited | Detailed, with account intelligence |
| **Customer proof** | Individual-reviewer testimonials only, no named enterprise logos found | Named case studies: Snowflake, monday.com, Seismic, OUTFRONT Media |
| **Integrations** | Broader but shallower list, largely Zapier-dependent for the long tail | Fewer but deeper native integrations, with signal-triggered automation (Salesloft Rhythm) |
| **Contract terms** | Annual, paid upfront; recurring reviewer complaints about cancellation friction | Annual only, auto-renewal; high price floor cited as a recurring complaint |
| **Capterra satisfaction** | 4.5/5 (570 verified reviews) | 4.1/5 (321 verified reviews) |
| **Recurring accuracy complaints** | Outdated emails/phone numbers, especially outside North America | Some reviewers report intent data as inconsistently accurate |

G2 is messier for both: G2 blocks automated fetch for each product page, so the numbers above are search-indexed snapshots, not confirmed live pulls, and should be re-checked before publishing anywhere that treats them as current. ZoomInfo's claim of ranking #1 in 142 G2 Spring 2026 category reports comes from its own investor-relations press release, which could not be independently confirmed (the release and its BusinessWire mirror both failed to load during research); treat that specific claim as self-reported until verified. Review volume aside, the two platforms serve different buyers, so a rating gap is not the same as one tool being objectively better for every use case.
