  heading: "The short answer"
  intro: >-
    Profound and Otterly.AI are both AI visibility (AEO) trackers founded in
    2024, and both monitor how brands show up in AI answers across ChatGPT,
    Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The honest split is depth versus
    price. Profound is the broader, more automated platform: it tracks up to
    nine AI engines on its Enterprise tier (including Meta AI and DeepSeek,
    which Otterly does not track at all), layers on agentic "Agents" and
    weekly "Aim" recommendations beyond passive monitoring, and publishes
    SSO/SAML plus SOC2 on its Enterprise tier. Otterly.AI is the leaner,
    cheaper entry point: its Lite plan starts at $29/mo and still covers four
    core engines, well below Profound's $99/mo Starter, which covers ChatGPT
    only, and Otterly also ships its own Agent Analytics module on Standard and
    above. Both vendors have public facts with real gaps and figures that vary
    by source, flagged plainly below rather than smoothed over.

faqs:
  - q: "What is the difference between Profound and Otterly.AI?"
    a: >-
      Profound is the broader, more automated AEO platform: it tracks up to
      nine AI engines on its top tier, including Meta AI and DeepSeek, and adds
      agentic workflow features (Agents plus weekly Aim recommendations) beyond
      monitoring. Otterly.AI is a leaner AI search monitoring and GEO-audit
      tool that costs far less at every public tier and covers four core engines

      DeepSeek at any tier and has no autonomous agent layer. Both vendors ship
      an Agent Analytics module for AI bot and agent traffic.

    a: >-

      need the widest engine ceiling, agentic automation, or published
      enterprise compliance (SSO/SAML/SOC2) for a regulated buyer. Otterly.AI
      is the stronger pick if you want broad core-engine coverage at a low
      entry price, built-in content/GEO audits, and a higher headline G2 score,
      though on a much smaller review sample than Profound's. The right answer
      depends on budget and whether you need an agent layer or accurate
      monitoring at the lowest cost.
