## How to use this checklist

Work top to bottom the first time you build or take over an account, then treat it as a recurring health check. The foundations section matters most, time zone, currency, and conversion tracking are hard or impossible to fix once campaigns are built on top of them.

## Get the foundations right

Set the correct time zone and currency before building anything, since neither can be changed after account creation. Confirm conversion tracking actually works and is verified before you spend a dollar, link GA4, Search Console, and your CRM where relevant, and secure account access with 2-step verification so a single compromised login cannot take down the account.

## Choose the right campaign types

Use Search for high-intent capture, people actively looking for a solution like yours, and add Performance Max for expansion and keyword discovery once Search is stable and generating data. Display and Demand Gen work well for awareness and remarketing, not for cold prospecting, so match each campaign type to the funnel stage it actually serves instead of running everything at once from day one.

## Build the keyword and targeting layer

Separate Brand, Generic, and Competitor into distinct campaigns so their very different economics never blend together in one budget or bid strategy. Use a deliberate match-type strategy rather than defaulting everything to broad, build and maintain a shared negative keyword list across campaigns, and keep ad groups tightly themed around a small set of closely related keywords.

## Write ads and assets that convert

Write two to three responsive search ads per ad group with genuinely different angles, not minor variations of the same one, so the algorithm has real options to test. Add every relevant asset, sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, a lead form or call extension, and send every ad group to a message-matched landing page, never the homepage.

## Choose bidding and manage budget

Choose a bid strategy that matches your conversion volume and goal, Manual or Maximise Clicks early with limited data, then move to tCPA or tROAS once you have enough conversions for automated bidding to work with. Monitor budget pacing across campaigns so nothing is silently budget-limited and losing you volume you are already paying to compete for.

## Keep optimising, every week

Review the search-terms report weekly and add negatives before waste compounds, and test new ad copy and assets on a regular cadence rather than leaving winning ads untouched indefinitely. Refresh creative before performance decays from fatigue, and reallocate budget away from underperforming campaigns toward what is actually converting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Google Ads checklist?

A Google Ads checklist is a structured list of everything needed to run the platform well: account foundations, choosing campaign types, keyword and targeting strategy, ad and asset quality, bidding strategy, and the ongoing optimisation that keeps an account performing instead of decaying quietly.

### How often should I review my Google Ads account?

Review the search-terms report and key metrics weekly, and run a full structural review quarterly or whenever performance shifts unexpectedly. Weekly checks catch wasted spend fast; the quarterly review catches bigger issues like a bid strategy that no longer fits your data or a budget split that no longer matches performance.

### What is the difference between Google Ads setup and Google Ads management?

Setup is the one-time work of building an account correctly: foundations, tracking, campaign structure, keywords, and ads. Management is what happens after launch, monitoring the search-terms report, testing creative, adjusting bids and budgets, and refreshing what has gone stale. A well-managed account run on a badly set-up foundation still underperforms.

### Should I use automated or manual bidding in Google Ads?

Start with manual bidding or Maximise Clicks while an account has little conversion data, since automated bidding needs enough conversions to learn from. Move to tCPA or tROAS once you have a reliable volume of conversions, which for most B2B SaaS accounts means having enough data flowing in for the algorithm to optimise against reliably.

### What is the biggest mistake in running Google Ads?

Launching before conversion tracking is verified, so every subsequent decision, including automated bidding, is based on unreliable data. Close behind are leaving Search Partners and broad match on by default, blending Brand and Generic budgets, and letting creative and keyword lists go untouched for months at a time.
