## How to use this checklist

ChatGPT reaches your content in two ways: its own crawlers and its search feature (which leans on the Bing index). Make sure both can find you, then focus on being the most citable, most-trusted source for your topic.

## Let ChatGPT reach you

Allow OpenAI's crawlers in robots.txt: GPTBot (training), OAI-SearchBot (search), and ChatGPT-User (browsing). Make sure content is server-rendered so non-JS crawlers can read it, and keep the site fast so retrieval can fetch pages. (For the full crawler picture, use the [AI crawler access checklist](/checklists/ai-crawler-access-checklist/).)

## Be in the index ChatGPT search uses

ChatGPT's search feature leans heavily on Bing, so verify and optimise your site in Bing Webmaster Tools, submit your sitemap, use IndexNow for fast pickup, and confirm your key pages are actually indexed by Bing. Being invisible in Bing means being invisible to ChatGPT search.

## Be the most citable answer

Front-load a clear, quotable answer in the first one or two sentences of each page, state specific facts, numbers, and dates a model can lift confidently, publish original data and definitions, and structure content as clean question-and-answer blocks.

## Build entity authority

Keep your entity facts consistent everywhere, earn mentions on the sources ChatGPT trusts (Wikipedia, Reddit, G2, reputable press), and get listed in best-of and comparison articles alongside category leaders. ChatGPT recommends brands it has seen described consistently and positively across the web.

## Measure

Test your target prompts in ChatGPT monthly and note whether you are mentioned or cited, track ChatGPT referral traffic in GA4, and double down on the pages and sources that get you surfaced.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is ChatGPT SEO?

ChatGPT SEO is optimising your brand and content to be surfaced and cited inside ChatGPT. It combines allowing OpenAI's crawlers, being present in the Bing index (which ChatGPT search uses), publishing highly citable content, and building the consistent entity authority that makes ChatGPT reference and recommend you.

### How do I get my website into ChatGPT?

Allow OpenAI's crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User) in robots.txt, make sure your pages are indexed in Bing since ChatGPT search relies on it, and publish clear, citable, well-sourced content. Then reinforce it with consistent entity information and mentions on the trusted sites ChatGPT draws from.

### Does ChatGPT use Google or Bing?

ChatGPT's built-in search feature primarily uses the Bing index, not Google. That is why appearing in Bing Webmaster Tools and getting your pages indexed by Bing is a key, and often overlooked, part of ChatGPT SEO. Its training data also spans a broad crawl of the web.

### Should I block GPTBot?

Only if you have a specific reason to keep your content out of AI training and are willing to reduce your visibility. If you want ChatGPT to surface and cite you, allow GPTBot and especially OAI-SearchBot. Most brands chasing AI-search visibility should allow them.

### How is ChatGPT SEO different from GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the broad discipline of being cited across all generative assistants. ChatGPT SEO is the platform-specific execution for ChatGPT, which has its own mechanics: OpenAI's crawlers and a dependence on the Bing index. Do the GEO fundamentals, then apply the ChatGPT specifics.
