## How to use this checklist

Because Copilot draws on Bing, most of the work is Bing SEO that many teams neglect. Get indexed and ranking in Bing first, then apply the answer-first and authority layers.

## Win Bing first

Verify and configure the site in Bing Webmaster Tools, submit your sitemap, and confirm your key pages are indexed by Bing. Use IndexNow to get new and updated pages picked up fast, and fix Bing-specific crawl and indexing issues in the Bing tools. Copilot cannot cite what Bing has not indexed.

## Let the crawlers in

Allow Bingbot and do not block Microsoft's AI crawlers in robots.txt, server-render content so it can be read without JavaScript, and keep the site fast and technically clean.

## Rank and answer well in Bing

Optimise for Bing's ranking factors (Bing weights on-page and exact-match signals a little differently from Google), front-load a clear, quotable answer, structure content as question-and-answer blocks with lists and tables, and win Bing featured snippets, which feed Copilot answers.

## Reinforce authority and entity signals

Keep your entity facts consistent across the web, earn mentions and links from sources Bing and Copilot trust, and add relevant, valid structured data.

## Measure

Track your Bing organic performance in Bing Webmaster Tools, test target prompts in Copilot and note whether you are cited, and monitor referral traffic to reinforce what gets surfaced.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Bing Copilot optimization?

Bing Copilot optimization is getting your brand and content surfaced in Microsoft Copilot, which is powered by the Bing search index. It centres on winning at Bing (indexing, ranking, IndexNow), allowing Microsoft's crawlers, and publishing citable, answer-first content that Copilot can reference.

### How do I get my site into Microsoft Copilot?

Get indexed and ranking in Bing, since Copilot draws on the Bing index. Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools, submit your sitemap, use IndexNow for fast pickup, allow Bingbot, and publish clear, citable content with valid schema. Strong Bing presence is the foundation of Copilot visibility.

### Does Copilot use Bing or Google?

Microsoft Copilot uses the Bing index, not Google. That makes Bing SEO, which many teams ignore in favour of Google, directly relevant to Copilot visibility. The same Bing presence also benefits ChatGPT's search feature, which leans on Bing too.

### What is IndexNow?

IndexNow is a protocol (backed by Microsoft/Bing) that lets you instantly notify search engines when pages are added or updated, so they are crawled and indexed faster. It is especially useful for Bing and, by extension, Copilot, because faster indexing means faster eligibility to be cited.

### Is Bing SEO worth it for B2B SaaS?

Increasingly yes, because Bing powers both Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT's search feature, and it is far less competitive than Google. Getting your key pages indexed and ranking in Bing is a low-effort, high-leverage way to expand into AI-assistant visibility that most competitors ignore.
