## How to use this checklist

Perplexity fetches and cites live pages, so crawler access and freshness come first, then citation-worthy content. Because it shows sources prominently, a well-sourced answer-first page is your best asset.

## Let Perplexity reach you

Allow PerplexityBot (and Perplexity-User) in robots.txt, server-render content so it can be read without JavaScript, and keep the site fast so real-time retrieval can fetch pages quickly. (For the full crawler picture, use the [AI crawler access checklist](/checklists/ai-crawler-access-checklist/).)

## Stay fresh and crawlable

Perplexity favours fresh, live pages, so publish and update regularly, keep an accurate sitemap so new pages are found fast, and fix crawl errors so your key pages stay indexable.

## Be citation-worthy

Front-load a direct, quotable answer in the first one or two sentences, cite your own sources with links (Perplexity rewards well-sourced pages), publish original data and clear definitions, and structure content as self-contained question-and-answer blocks.

## Build authority and entity signals

Keep your entity facts consistent across the web, earn mentions on the trusted sources Perplexity pulls from (Reddit, documentation, reputable press), and get listed in credible roundups and comparisons.

## Measure

Test your target prompts in Perplexity monthly and note citations, track Perplexity referral traffic in GA4, and double down on the pages and sources that get cited.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Perplexity SEO?

Perplexity SEO is optimising your content to be cited by Perplexity, an AI answer engine that searches the live web and shows its sources. It centres on allowing PerplexityBot, keeping pages fresh and crawlable, and publishing well-sourced, answer-first content that Perplexity can confidently cite.

### How do I get cited by Perplexity?

Allow PerplexityBot in robots.txt, keep your pages fresh and fast, and publish a clear, front-loaded answer backed by cited sources. Then build authority through consistent entity information and mentions on the trusted sites Perplexity draws from. Test your prompts and reinforce what already earns citations.

### Does Perplexity use its own crawler?

Yes. Perplexity uses PerplexityBot to index content and a Perplexity-User agent when it fetches a page in response to a live query. If you block these in robots.txt or via your CDN, you remove yourself from Perplexity's answers, so allow them if you want citations.

### Why does Perplexity favour fresh content?

Perplexity performs real-time web search to answer queries, so recently published or updated pages that directly address the question are more likely to be fetched and cited. Keeping high-value pages current and your sitemap accurate helps Perplexity find and prefer your content.

### How is Perplexity SEO different from ChatGPT SEO?

Both are generative-engine optimisation, but the mechanics differ: Perplexity leans on real-time crawling and prominent citations, while ChatGPT search leans on the Bing index. Perplexity rewards freshness and strong sourcing especially; ChatGPT rewards Bing presence and entity authority. The fundamentals overlap.
