A Meta Ads checklist covers what it takes to run Facebook and Instagram ads correctly: Business Manager foundation, Pixel and Conversions API tracking, campaign structure, audiences, creative, and honest measurement. Since Apple's iOS 14 tracking changes, getting the tracking layer right matters more than the targeting itself. This is the sequence we run for B2B SaaS clients running Meta at PipeRocket Digital.

It is interactive. Tick each item as you complete it, your progress saves in your browser, and you can download the whole thing as a PDF or Excel.

## How to use this checklist

Get the foundation and tracking sections fully working before you build a single campaign, since Meta's reporting has become far less reliable without both the Pixel and Conversions API running together. Everything after that, structure, audiences, creative, only performs as well as the data underneath it.

## Set up the account foundation

Set up Business Manager with your Ad Account, Page, and Pixel all connected properly, add a payment method, and set a daily or lifetime spend limit so a misconfigured campaign cannot run away with budget overnight. Verify your domain in Business Manager, since unverified domains lose access to certain conversion events, and secure admin access with 2-factor authentication and least-privilege roles for anyone who does not need full account access.

## Get tracking right

Install both the Meta Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI), relying on the Pixel alone since iOS 14 means losing a meaningful share of conversion data to tracking prevention. Configure Aggregated Event Measurement with up to eight prioritised events, confirm events are deduplicated between Pixel and CAPI so you are not double-counting, and test that every key conversion event actually fires before you spend real budget against it.

## Build the right campaign structure

Decide deliberately between Campaign Budget Optimisation (CBO), letting Meta allocate across ad sets, and manual ad set budgets, rather than defaulting to whichever Meta suggests. Match the campaign objective, Leads, Conversions, or Traffic, to your actual funnel goal, and keep prospecting campaigns separate from retargeting so reporting and budget stay easy to read.

## Build audiences that convert

Build custom audiences from your website visitors and CRM lists, and build lookalike audiences from your best existing customers rather than your entire customer list, quality of the seed audience matters more than its size. Check for excessive overlap between ad sets targeting similar audiences, since Meta's own auction will otherwise bid against itself.

## Create creative the algorithm can test

Provide multiple ad formats and genuinely different creatives per ad set, Meta's delivery system needs real variation to find what works, not five near-identical images. Send every ad to a message-matched landing page rather than a generic homepage, and refresh creative before frequency climbs and performance visibly fatigues.

## Measure and optimise honestly

Monitor frequency and CPM trends by campaign so rising costs do not go unnoticed until a monthly report. Compare Meta's own reported conversions against GA4 or CRM-verified numbers, since Meta's attribution tends to over-credit itself, and reallocate budget away from ad sets with a rising cost per result rather than letting them run on inertia.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Meta Ads checklist?

A Meta Ads checklist is a structured list of what it takes to run Facebook and Instagram ads well: Business Manager and Pixel setup, Conversions API tracking, campaign structure, audience building, creative variation, and measurement that accounts for Meta's own reporting biases.

### Why do I need both the Pixel and the Conversions API?

The Pixel alone misses a growing share of conversions due to browser tracking prevention and iOS 14 changes, since it depends on client-side signals a browser can block. The Conversions API sends data server-side instead, so running both together, with deduplication, gives Meta the most complete picture of what is actually converting.

### What is Campaign Budget Optimisation (CBO) in Meta Ads?

CBO lets Meta automatically distribute a single campaign budget across its ad sets toward whichever is performing best, rather than you fixing a manual budget per ad set. It works well when ad sets are targeting genuinely different, non-overlapping audiences; it works poorly when ad sets are too similar and end up competing with each other.

### Why does Meta over-report conversions?

Meta's attribution model tends to credit itself for conversions that may have happened anyway, or that another channel actually influenced more heavily, especially with a broad attribution window. Comparing Meta's native numbers against GA4 or CRM-verified conversions, and watching for gaps, gives a more honest read on what Meta is actually driving.

### How much creative variety do I need per ad set?

Provide at least three to five genuinely different creatives per ad set, not minor edits of the same image or headline, since Meta's delivery system needs real variation to learn what resonates. Refresh them once frequency climbs noticeably or performance starts declining, rather than waiting for a scheduled refresh date.
