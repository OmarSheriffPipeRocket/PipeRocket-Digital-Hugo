Local SEO is how you rank in local search results and the Google map pack: an optimised Business Profile, consistent citations, real reviews, and local signals on your site. This is the local checklist we run for location-based clients at PipeRocket Digital.

It is interactive. Tick each item as you finish it, your progress saves in your browser, and you can download the whole thing as a PDF.

## How to use this checklist

Start with your Google Business Profile, since it is the single biggest local ranking factor, then work outward to citations, reviews, and on-page signals. If you serve multiple locations, run the on-page section once per location.

## Own your Google Business Profile

Your Business Profile drives most of your map-pack visibility. Claim and verify it, choose the most accurate primary category plus relevant secondary ones, and complete every field: hours, services, attributes, description, and photos. Keep it active with fresh posts, products, and images, because an abandoned profile slips.

## Get NAP and citations consistent

Search engines cross-check your name, address, and phone number across the web, so keep them identical everywhere. Build and clean citations on the major data aggregators and relevant industry directories, fix duplicate or inconsistent listings, and add a Google Map embed plus LocalBusiness structured data on your site.

## Earn and manage reviews

Reviews are both a ranking factor and the deciding factor for many searchers. Earn a steady, genuine stream of Google reviews (never buy fakes), respond to every one, and build reviews on the platforms that matter in your industry.

## Add local signals on your site

Create a dedicated, unique page for each location you serve (not thin duplicates), work city and region terms naturally into titles, headings, and copy, and publish local content like area guides, local case studies, and community involvement.

## Build local authority

Local links carry real weight. Earn them from local news, chambers of commerce, and community sites, sponsor or partner with local organisations for genuine mentions, and get listed in reputable local and niche directories.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the practice of optimising to rank in location-based searches and the Google map pack. It centres on your Google Business Profile, consistent name-address-phone citations across the web, reviews, and location-specific signals on your website, so nearby customers find you when they search.

### What is the most important local SEO ranking factor?

A complete, accurate, and active Google Business Profile is the biggest single factor, followed by proximity to the searcher, review quantity and quality, and NAP consistency across citations. On-page relevance and local links reinforce all of that.

### How do I rank in the Google map pack?

Fully optimise and verify your Business Profile, choose the right categories, keep NAP consistent everywhere, earn a steady stream of genuine reviews and respond to them, and support it with location pages and local links. Proximity to the searcher also matters and is outside your control.

### How important are reviews for local SEO?

Very. Reviews influence both your local ranking and whether searchers choose you over competitors. A steady flow of authentic, recent reviews and thoughtful responses to all of them is one of the highest-leverage local SEO activities.

### Does local SEO matter for B2B SaaS?

Usually not, if you sell purely online with no physical location or local service area. Local SEO matters when you have offices, run local events, or serve specific regions. Most pure-play SaaS brands should invest in the broader SEO, technical, and off-page checklists instead.
