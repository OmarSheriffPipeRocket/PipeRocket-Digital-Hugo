Google AI Overviews (AIO) are the AI-generated answers at the top of Google results, and they mostly draw from pages that already rank well and answer the query cleanly. Optimising for them is the Google-specific execution of our broader [AEO checklist](/checklists/aeo-checklist/). This is the AIO checklist we run for clients at PipeRocket Digital.

## How to use this checklist

The order matters: rank first, then structure the page so AIO can lift a clean answer. Unlike standalone assistants, AI Overviews pull heavily from the top organic results, so classic ranking is the entry ticket.

## Earn the foundation by ranking first

AI Overviews mostly cite pages already ranking on page one, so the first job is to rank: match the search intent precisely, make sure the page is indexed and crawlable, and pass Core Web Vitals. If you are not in the top results, you are unlikely to be pulled into the overview.

## Answer the query and its fan-out

Google fans a query out into related sub-questions when it builds an overview. Front-load a direct, quotable answer in the first one or two sentences, cover those sub-questions on the page, structure content as clear question-and-answer blocks, and use lists and tables for steps and comparisons.

## Structure for extraction

Use the real question as the heading with the answer directly beneath, add FAQ and HowTo structured data where relevant, keep the answer content server-rendered rather than JavaScript-only, and win the featured snippet, which frequently feeds the AI Overview.

## Reinforce authority and freshness

Support claims with specific numbers, dates, and cited sources, show clear E-E-A-T (named author, credentials, first-hand experience), and keep content fresh so it stays eligible as overviews regenerate.

## Measure

Watch impression and click shifts in Search Console as AI Overviews appear for your queries, check which queries trigger an overview and whether you are cited, and protect high-value pages by keeping them the best available answer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews (AIO) are AI-generated summaries shown at the top of some search results, synthesised from multiple web pages with links to the sources. They aim to answer the query directly, which can reduce clicks to the blue links below, so being cited in the overview is increasingly valuable.

### How do I optimise for Google AI Overviews?

Rank on page one first, since overviews draw mainly from top results, then structure the page to be lifted: front-load a direct answer, cover the related sub-questions Google fans out, add FAQ/HowTo schema, win the featured snippet, and reinforce authority and freshness. There is no separate opt-in beyond strong SEO plus answer-first structure.

### Do I need special schema for AI Overviews?

No special schema is required to appear in AI Overviews, per Google's own guidance. However, FAQ, HowTo, and Article structured data make your content easier to parse and eligible for rich results, so it is recommended. The larger levers are ranking well and answering the query clearly.

### Why did my traffic drop when AI Overviews appeared?

AI Overviews can answer the query on the results page, reducing clicks for informational searches even when you are cited. Protect traffic by targeting queries with real click intent, being the cited source (which still earns visibility), and strengthening bottom-of-funnel pages where users need to visit your site.

### What is the difference between AI Overviews and AEO?

AI Overviews are one specific Google surface. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the broader discipline of getting cited across all answer engines, including snippets, People Also Ask, and AI Overviews. This checklist is the AIO-specific execution within the wider AEO approach.
