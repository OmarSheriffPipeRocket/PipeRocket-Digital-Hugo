SEO competitor analysis is how you reverse-engineer why other sites outrank you and turn it into your own roadmap: keyword gaps, content gaps, backlink gaps, and the SERP features they win. This is the competitive analysis our team runs for clients at PipeRocket Digital.

It is interactive. Tick each item as you finish it, your progress saves in your browser, and you can download the whole thing as a PDF.

## How to use this checklist

Identify the right competitors first, because your search competitors are often not your business competitors. Then work through the gap analyses and end with a prioritised roadmap, not a pile of interesting-but-unused data.

## Identify the right competitors

List your business competitors and your search competitors separately, because they frequently differ. Confirm your true search competitors by who actually ranks for your target keywords, then pick three to five to benchmark in depth.

## Find the keyword gap

Run a keyword gap analysis to surface the terms competitors rank for and you do not. Segment those gaps by intent and funnel stage, then prioritise by business value and difficulty so you chase winnable, valuable terms first.

## Find the content gap

Map competitors' top pages by traffic and the topics they cover, spot the topics and formats you are missing, and note where you can cover a topic more completely or with better first-hand experience than they do.

## Find the backlink gap

Compare referring domains to see which links competitors have that you do not, study their most-linked pages to reverse-engineer the assets that earned those links, and build a realistic target list of link sources from their profile.

## Benchmark the SERP and turn it into action

Analyse which SERP features competitors win (snippets, People Also Ask, AI Overviews, video), compare titles, depth, and freshness on head-to-head keywords, and benchmark their technical setup and Core Web Vitals. Then prioritise every gap into a ranked roadmap by impact and effort, assign owners, and set a re-benchmark cadence.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is SEO competitor analysis?

SEO competitor analysis is the process of studying the sites that outrank you to understand why, then using those insights to improve your own strategy. It covers identifying your real search competitors and analysing the keyword, content, backlink, and SERP-feature gaps between you and them.

### How do I find my SEO competitors?

Your SEO competitors are the sites that rank for your target keywords, which are often different from your direct business competitors. Search your priority keywords and note who consistently appears, or use a keyword tool's competing-domains report, then shortlist three to five to analyse in depth.

### What is a keyword gap analysis?

A keyword gap analysis compares your rankings against competitors to surface valuable terms they rank for and you do not. It is one of the fastest ways to build a prioritised content roadmap, because it shows proven demand your competitors are already capturing.

### How often should I analyse competitors?

Do a deep competitor analysis quarterly, and a lighter check whenever you plan a new content push or notice a ranking shift. Markets and SERPs change, so a once-and-done analysis goes stale fast.

### What tools do I need for SEO competitor analysis?

A keyword and backlink tool such as Ahrefs or Semrush covers the keyword, content, and backlink gap analyses. Google Search Console shows your own baseline, and manually reviewing the live SERP reveals the features and content patterns competitors use to win specific queries.
