SaaS link building is the work of earning links that move rankings for a software company: auditing what you have, building assets worth citing, running digital PR, and winning the product-led and partnership links only a SaaS can earn. ## How to use this checklist

Link building compounds an already-solid site; it does not rescue a broken one. Get on-page and technical foundations in place first, then earn links to your money pages and pillar content. For the wider off-page picture, use the [off-page SEO checklist](/checklists/off-page-seo-checklist/).

## Audit and clean before you build

You cannot improve what you have not measured. Audit your current backlink profile and referring domains, benchmark against the competitors you want to outrank, fix or disavow genuinely toxic links, and decide which money pages and pillar content deserve the links you are about to earn.

## Build assets worth linking to

Nobody links to a product page on request; they link to something useful. Publish original data, research, and surveys people cite, build free tools and templates that earn links passively, and create definitive guides worth referencing in your category. These assets do the heavy lifting for every campaign that follows.

## Earn coverage with digital PR

Digital PR turns assets into links at authoritative sites. Pitch data-led stories to journalists and industry publications, earn expert commentary and quotes in relevant articles, and run genuine outreach for guest posts on real, relevant sites rather than low-quality link farms.

## Win links only SaaS can earn

SaaS has link sources most businesses do not. Get listed in relevant software directories and marketplaces, earn integration and partner-page links from the tools you integrate with, and get included in "best of" and alternative roundups alongside your category peers.

## Turn brand mentions into links

Much of your link opportunity already exists as unlinked mentions. Monitor for brand mentions and convert them to links, earn citations on Reddit, forums, and communities your buyers trust, and keep your NAP and entity data consistent across profiles so the mentions reinforce one clear entity.

## Measure links that move rankings

Not all links matter equally. Track referring domains, authority, and links pointing to your money pages, watch for and reclaim lost links, and tie link growth back to rankings on the target pages so you invest in what actually moves the needle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is link building hard for SaaS companies?

SaaS products are often abstract and B2B, so they lack the natural link magnets that consumer or local businesses enjoy. Winning links usually requires creating linkable assets (data, tools, research), running digital PR, and leaning on SaaS-specific sources like integration pages and software directories, which takes more deliberate effort.

### What are the best link building tactics for SaaS?

The most durable tactics are publishing original data and research, building free tools that earn links passively, running digital PR to authoritative publications, earning integration and partner links, getting into category roundups, and reclaiming unlinked brand mentions. These beat mass outreach for low-quality links, which risks more than it returns.

### How many backlinks does a SaaS site need?

There is no fixed number; relevance and authority matter far more than volume. The practical benchmark is the referring-domain profile of the pages currently outranking you for your target keywords. Match and then exceed the quality of those linking domains to your money pages rather than chasing a raw count.

### Should SaaS companies buy backlinks?

Buying links violates Google's guidelines and risks manual actions or devaluation, so it is not a strategy we use or recommend. The durable alternative is earning links through assets, digital PR, and product-led sources, which hold their value and do not put your rankings at risk.
