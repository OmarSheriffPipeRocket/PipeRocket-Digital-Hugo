Consultant or agency?

When to hire an SEO consultant vs an SEO agency

Both grow your organic search. The difference is who does the doing. A consultant sets the strategy and upskills your team; an agency runs the execution end to end. Here is the honest split.

Hire Kim as your SEO consultant when

  • You have a content or marketing team that can execute, but not the SEO strategy to direct them.
  • You want senior, direct access to a 15-year specialist, not an account manager.
  • Your organic growth has plateaued and you need a diagnosis of why.
  • You want to build SEO into a repeatable in-house capability.
  • You need a fractional SEO lead for a defined period, not a full retainer.

Hire the SEO agency when

  • You want the SEO work executed for you, content, technical, and link building.
  • You do not have the internal bandwidth to run the strategy yourselves.
  • You want a full team of writers, technical SEOs, and link builders on the program.
  • You want one accountable partner owning the outcome, not just the plan.
  • You need execution at a pace an advisory relationship cannot match.

Not sure which fits? Kim will tell you honestly on the first call, including when an agency retainer is the better spend.
