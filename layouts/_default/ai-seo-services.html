How AI SEO services turn AI conversations into pipeline

AI SEO has two halves: AEO services (Answer Engine Optimization) make your brand the cited answer, and GEO services (Generative Engine Optimization) make it visible across every generative AI experience. We run both as one program.

Cited, not just ranked

Ranking #1 on Google means nothing if ChatGPT or Perplexity quotes a competitor in the answer. AEO is about being the source AI engines cite when your buyer asks a buying-stage question. We engineer your content to be the citation, not the also-ran.

Question-shaped content

AI engines reward content that answers a specific question, cleanly, with sourced claims. We restructure your content around buyer questions, schema, citations, retrieval-friendly framing, so the answer is yours every time someone asks.

Train the engines, not just rank for them

AI training corpora, retrieval-augmented generation indexes, citation graphs, the inputs AI uses to decide what’s authoritative. We build distribution that lands your brand in the right places: Reddit, GitHub, Wikipedia-grade sources, podcasts, and trusted directories.

Pipeline over rankings

Citations are a means. Pipeline is the outcome. We track AI-citation share of voice across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and AI Overviews, but every monthly review centres on demos booked from AI-led referrals and pipeline influenced.

The PipeRocket AEO & GEO Approach

Each phase is built around how AI engines retrieve, evaluate, and cite content, not how Google ranked content in 2018.

Audit AI visibility

We measure your current citation share of voice across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini for the buyer questions that matter. Baseline first. Anything we can’t measure, we can’t move.

Map buyer questions

We work with sales to map the actual questions your buyers ask AI assistants, comparison, alternatives, integration, pricing, "is X better than Y for Z". Each becomes a target prompt with a target citation outcome.

Engineer for retrieval

Content gets restructured for AI retrieval, claim-source pairing, factual density, schema, retrieval-friendly headings, citation-grade quotes. AI engines find a clean answer to quote, with your brand attached.

Land in training corpora

AI engines train and retrieve from specific corpora. Reddit, Wikipedia-grade sources, GitHub, podcast transcripts, trade press. We earn placements there, not just on high-DR domains AI engines ignore.

Build trust-signal posture

AI engines weight authoritativeness, author profiles, brand entities, citation graphs, structured data. We strengthen the posture that earns citations: real bios, schema entities, consistent E-A-T signals across the corpus.

Track citations weekly

We monitor citation share of voice across the AI engines weekly, by buyer question, by competitor. Movements get diagnosed and acted on before they cost you pipeline.

Wire AI-led pipeline

Referrers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude get tagged in your CRM. Demos and pipeline from AI-led sessions get tracked separately so you can quantify what AI visibility is actually worth.

Compound vs. Google

Everything we ship for AI engines also strengthens classic SEO, schema, citation graphs, factual density. The investment compounds across both worlds, so you don’t pick a winner.
